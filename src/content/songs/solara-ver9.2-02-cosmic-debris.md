CONCEPT

Crew reaches a dead star where the Hollow just passed. Millions displaced -- an entire galaxy lost. SOLARA offers them her system. Castor says: put them where Pollux is. Pollux is just learning to be a star. Emotional, heavy with the sadness of losing a home, with a hint of hope.

Heavy and sad with a hint of hope, slow tempo, strings and choir, SOLARA's voice carrying the grief and the offer, Castor's line lands as the emotional turn, BPM 72, minor key. 4:45
