[Quiet piano, echoing hall acoustics, distant wind]

### Verse 1
The last wall has fallen, the throne is secured
I've taken the kingdom, the outcome ensured
Every horror displayed in its rightful position
Every soul in my realm showing total submission
I stand at the top of the highest dark tower
Holding the total, absolute power
Yet the silence inside of this victory space
Leaves an unanswered question upon my face

### Pre-Chorus
I conquered the world, I broke every thread
So why is that red-haired light in my head?

### Chorus
Is this the end of the beginning?
Or the beginning of the end?
I won every battle I came to defend
I built my kingdom on ruin and fright
So why am I searching the dark for a light?
Is this the end of the beginning...
Or where it begins?

### Verse 2
Down on the horizon, across the grey plain
Someone is standing who doesn't feel pain
She doesn't bow down, she doesn't turn cold
She carries a warmth that is ancient and bold
She looks through the mist with a sword in her hand
And steps toward the tower where I rule the land
For the very first time, someone refuses to yield
An unanswered question upon the field

### Pre-Chorus 2
Recognition exists before understanding...
Who are you standing there?

### Chorus
Is this the end of the beginning?
Or the beginning of the end?
I won every battle I came to defend

### Bridge
[Gothic opera meets sci-fi chimes]
You don't bow.
You don't run.
You don't bleed out.
You just... stand there.
Looking at me like you know my name.

### Final Chorus
Is this the end of the beginning?
Or the spark in the night?
The Dark Queen has met her first equal in light!
The box is wide open, the cycle turns round
And two separate sisters have finally found...
Each other.

### Outro — whispered, fading out
Is this the end of the beginning?
Or the beginning of the end?
...Come closer.
