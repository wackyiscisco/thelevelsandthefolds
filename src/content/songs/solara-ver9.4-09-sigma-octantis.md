CONCEPT

After reading Pollux's notes, SOLARA decides to visit the South -- Sigma Octantis (Pandora's star). The journey is the song: old world healed, flowers, children laughing, people thanking her for what Pandora and Elara built. She feels a deep connection. They arrive at Sigma Octantis -- it's hurting. She hears a cry for help. She starts running.
