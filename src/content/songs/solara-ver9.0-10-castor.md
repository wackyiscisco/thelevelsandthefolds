[Verse 1]

There was someone at the garden gate

I didn't feel a threat

I felt something older

Something I had not expected yet

He said: my brother sends his warmest words

He cannot come, not yet

He is learning how to be a star

And stars need time to set

He said: my name is Castor

And I come to bend the knee

I come because the age has turned

And a new One now must be

[Pre-Chorus]

And I looked at him

Standing in the light of the garden

And I thought:

How long has it been

Since someone came without an agenda

Without a war

Without a debt that had to clear?

He offered me his allegiance

His arm

His sword

His name

And I said:

Put the sword away

That is not why you came

[Chorus]

I do not walk in front

And I do not walk behind

I do not need a soldier

I need someone

I can talk to about things

That the council cannot hold

Castor

You crossed the dark to reach me

Because your brother cannot yet

Pollux sends his warmest words

Through you

And I receive them

But what I need is company

Not loyalty

Not a knight on bended knee

Not a war commander

Not someone sworn to fall for me

Sit down

Tell me about your brother

Tell me what he did to earn the sky

Sit here in the garden

And just talk to me

The way that no one

Has since I arrived

[Verse 2]

So he sat

And the Raven watched from the ancient tree

And Castor told me everything:

How Pollux chose the stars

Over anything that would have lasted

How heroism isn't loud

It's just a choice made fast and clear

How when the offer came to keep his light forever

He said: not without him

Give it to us both

Or I'm not here

And I listened

The way you listen to a story

That explains

Something you didn't have a name for

[Pre-Chorus 2]

And I thought:

That is what the twins do

They do not leave the other behind

That is what this whole saga

Has always been

Two lights that cannot rest

Until they find the other

Across every line

And Castor told me:

Pollux will come when he can stand in starlight steady

He will come

And when he does

He will bring what you need

He wanted me to say:

He is already yours

[Chorus]

[Bridge — SOLARA, quiet, to herself]

I have sat at the council table

I have held the order in both hands

I have felt the Hollow breathing

In the dark across the lands

And I have not said once

To anyone

How much I carry

When no one is watching

But Castor stayed until the evening

And the Raven kept him company

And for one afternoon in the garden

I was just

Someone with a friend

Who understood what twins could be

The Hollow is still building

The council is still planning

And I will need more than company soon

But this

This was the first of them

The first one who came walking

Not to kneel

But to sit with me and talk

About his brother

Who shines

[Final Chorus]

I do not walk in front

And I do not walk behind

But the Hollow has no conscience

And I cannot face it

On my own this time

Castor

You came for your brother

And stayed because I asked

Tell Pollux when he learns to move in starlight

Tell him what I said:

The garden has a table

And the Raven keeps the seat

And when he is ready

I will be here

And I will need more knights

Before this ends

[Outro — SOLARA looks at the sky where Pollux shines]

He stood to leave at nightfall.

I walked him to the gate.

He looked up at the star his brother was becoming.

I looked too.

And I said:

Tell him I will wait.

And Castor smiled.

And that was enough

For today.
