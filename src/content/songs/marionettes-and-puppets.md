### Verse 1
She moves without making a sound
Hands like weather, wrists like vows
The room rearranges at her feet
And every secret changes how it bows
You think you hold the thread of her
But she holds the way the strings unwind
And every puppet in your theater
Feels the gravity of her design

### Pre-Chorus
She learned to read the hands that move
She learned to find the place they pull
She watches them perform their parts
And smiles when they unspool

### Chorus
Marionettes and puppets
Dancing on the edge of her smile
Marionettes and puppets
Thinking they'll control a while
But every string she touches
Is a map to something truer
Marionettes and puppets
She doesn't need them to be newer

### Verse 2
You trace the pattern of her steps
You mark the moments she decides
You raise your glass to celebrate
The show you think you will not mind
But when the curtain falls for real
You find your hands are suddenly free
Except there's nothing left to hold
Except the memory of her and me

### Bridge
The thing about the strings
Is that they taught themselves to sing
And now the audience is clapping
To the echo of the thing

### Final Chorus
Marionettes and puppets
Dance until the daylight frays
Marionettes and puppets
Break whenever she says
And you'll remember how you danced
To the way she turned the light
Marionettes and puppets
We learned to move by sight
