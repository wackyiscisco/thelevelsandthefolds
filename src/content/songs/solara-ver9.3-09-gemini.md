[Verse 1]
The gate from thirty-four had opened
Into the highest level any of them had stood on
And it had taken approximately four minutes
For it to go wrong

The Hunter said something
That was meant for the Hero
That was heard by the King

The Hero said something
That was meant to fix it
That made it significantly worse

The King said something to his guards
That required no translation

[Pre-Chorus]
Castor said: run
They ran
The Swan ran with them
And then
He stopped

[Chorus]
He turned around
He walked back
He stood before the King of Level thirty-five
And he said:
Your Majesty
I am asking for one thing
Give me the floor
Let me show you something
And if it is not worth your time
I will leave with them and never return

Give me the floor

[Verse 2]
The King said: you have the floor

And the Swan danced

Not to escape
Not to distract
Not to survive

He danced because this was what he was
What he had always been
What The Old The One had tried to make him forget
Along with the name

He danced the way Level thirty-four had taught him to dance
With Phaethon's song underneath
The sun chariot falling
And something catching it

The King watched
The guards watched
The whole level went quiet

[Pre-Chorus 2]
Castor had stopped running
He was watching from the gate

He did not know why this moment felt important
More important than the running
More important than anything
He had felt since the corona flash

He stood very still
And watched

[Bridge]
When the Swan finished
The crowd was quiet for one breath

And then a voice from the back said:

I know you
I know who you are

I have seen you dance
In the old records
Before the names were taken

You are Cygnus

And the Swan stood completely still
Not weighted — lighter
Like something that had been missing
Had come back to exactly the place it belonged

His name was Cygnus
He had always known that
He had forgotten
He remembered now

[Final Chorus]
He bowed to the King
The King bowed back
Which did not happen often

The door appeared
The way doors appear
When a level decides you have earned passage

Cygnus walked through
He turned once
He bowed again

[Outro]
Castor went last

He looked at the place where Cygnus had danced
He thought about names
And what it cost to lose one
And what it felt like to hear it back from a stranger

On the other side:
The Hero said: what just happened
The Hunter said: I think he got his name back
The Hero said: what was it
The Hunter said: Cygnus
The Hero said: that's a good name
The Hunter said: better than nothing

They both looked at each other
Neither of them said anything else
