**[Verse 1]**

Six in the morning, the old bells complain
Curtains pulled open, let gold through the pane
One little stretch and a crack of the spine
Another immortal beginning on time

Twenty-five push-ups, then twenty-five more
Sit-ups while staring at cracks in the floor
Centuries pass but the discipline stays
Eternity's better with excellent abs anyway

**[Pre-Chorus]**

Mirror, mirror, nothing strange here
Perfect reflection, remarkably clear
Turn to the left, now turn to the right
Yes, still devastating in morning light

**[Chorus]**

Open the windows, throw wide every door
Let all that daylight come flooding the floor
No cloak, no casket, no reason to hide
The morning looks better with me outside

Let every shadow crawl back where it's been
I'm taking the mountain with coffee and grin
Whatever old stories insist I should do
They've clearly never met someone this good-looking at six-oh-two

**[Verse 2]**

Brush every fang till the ivory gleams
Trim up the beard with meticulous schemes
One final glance and a confident nod
If beauty's a curse, then I suffer a lot

Three gentle knocks and old Fredo appears
Faithful as ever for thousands of years
Silver tray steady, expression severe
Coffee so fragrant it resurrects the dead around here

“Morning, my Count.”

“Fredo.”

A sip.

Then another.

A pause for respect.

“Delicious.”

Fredo has heard this for centuries
And somehow looks thoroughly unimpressed

**[Pre-Chorus]**

Fresh bread waiting, pastries in rows
Butter still melting wherever it goes
Eggs from the kitchen, perfectly done
Breakfast tastes better when served with the sun

**[Chorus]**

Open the windows, throw wide every door
Let all that daylight come flooding the floor
No cloak, no casket, no reason to hide
The morning looks better with me outside

Let every shadow crawl back where it's been
I'm taking the mountain with coffee and grin
Whatever old stories insist I should do
They're welcome to whisper
I've breakfast to chew

**[Bridge]**

Two hundred centuries, Fredo has stayed
Serving this castle before I was made
He knew my parents, or so I have heard
Ask him about them and he guards every word

Perhaps one day
Perhaps not today

There are pastries remaining
And mysteries keep

Some questions improve
When you leave them asleep

**[Break]**

Boots on the stone
Coat on the shoulder
Sun climbing higher
Morning growing older

The ancient front doors groan open wide

And nothing catches fire.

How disappointing.

**[Verse 3]**

Down through the courtyard where old gargoyles stare
Past weathered towers and cold mountain air
Servants are stirring somewhere deep inside
But none worth disturbing on such a fine stride

The castle behind me has stood beyond count
Perched like a crown on the edge of the mount
Below lies the world through the thinning white haze
Valleys and forests catching the blaze

I breathe in slowly
Survey it all

A thousand roads
A thousand halls

Creatures awakening far down below
Living their lives where the cold rivers flow

And here I remain
At the top of it all

Naturally.

**[Final Chorus]**

Let morning pour over the towers and stone
Let sunlight discover the places unknown
No smoke from my shoulders, no blistering skin
Just warmth on my face and an irritating grin

The legends can argue, the old myths can whine
The daylight is lovely and breakfast was fine
The whole world is waking beneath my estate

And somehow I suspect
It will all tempt fate

**[Outro]**

Count Wrongula smiles.

Hands behind his back.

Sunlight across his face.

The valley beneath him.

A sip from the cup Fredo packed for the walk.

“Hmm.”

Another sip.

“Today…”

He surveys the entire world below.

“…is going to be a very good day.”

And somewhere far beneath the mountain,

something disagrees.
