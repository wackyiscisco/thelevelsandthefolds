[Intro — open road, stillness, then seven figures]
There were seven shapes on the road
Not moving
Just there

[Verse 1]
My hand was at the hilt before I thought
The riders spread beside me, same instinct
Seven figures standing in the middle of the road
No weapons drawn
No armor
No intent I recognized
They weren't the kind of seven I had studied
They weren't the kind of seven I had faced
They looked like ordinary strangers
Except for their eyes
Their eyes were old

[Pre-Chorus 1]
I gave the signal — hold
Something told me
Battle was the wrong answer for once
Something in the way they waited
Not braced for impact
Just patient
Just certain

[Chorus]
Seven strangers on the road to somewhere I had never been
Seven faces carrying a weight I hadn't learned to read
I have faced the kind that breaks you
I have faced the kind that burns
But I had never faced the kind
That simply —
Knew.

[Verse 2]
One of them stepped forward
Not a threat
Not a warning
Something closer to an offering
I asked who they were
She said the answer wasn't simple
She told me what I had been fighting was the floor
Not the ceiling
Not even close

[Pre-Chorus 2]
I had burned down cathedrals
I had crossed every name off every list
I thought I understood the shape of the war
I did not know the war had a shape
I had never been shown

[Chorus 2]
Seven strangers on the road to somewhere I had never been
Seven faces with a warning I was not prepared to keep
They were not here for what I'd done
They were here for what was coming
And what was coming
Didn't know my name

[Bridge — harp and angel hymns]
Patience named The Watchers
Every war recorded — including mine

Humility said angels
Was the smallest word they had

Diligence said the Council
Had logged it all before it reached the ground

Chastity said what I've been fighting
Was only the surface of what exists

Then Patience said I was not close
And they were not certain I should be

[Outro — Marching beat]
They were gone before I could answer
No direction I could follow
No road back to what I knew before they spoke
I stood there with everything I thought I had figured out
Suddenly the smallest part
Of something I had no name for

We said nothing -

We rode - north.
