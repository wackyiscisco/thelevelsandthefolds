[Verse 1]
The door was open when I walked through
No one closed it when he fell
The corridor is breathing something older
Than the prayers they used to sell
Something scuttles in the upper shadow
Something drags itself along the floor
I have walked in dark my whole existence
But I have never felt a dark like this before

[Pre-Chorus]
And then — a pulse
Like someone lit a candle somewhere west of here
She's coming
She's already near

[Chorus]
I reached for dark and found a thread of white
My hand went out and something blazing came to light
I did not know what I was holding
I did not know it would be bright
But everything that moved toward me scattered
And I stood there in the wreckage of my own new sight
Corridors and spirals — I am learning on my feet
Finding what I carry as I clear the path between

[Verse 2]
I tried again and this time it was different
The light did not burst out — it pulled and bent
Like water learning which way it should travel
I aimed it at the dark ahead and it went
It left a warmth along the stairwell stonework
A kind of light I have not carried in years
I filed it somewhere quiet for remembering
And kept on moving through the corridors and stairs

[Pre-Chorus]
And then — a pulse
Stronger now, she's crossing something somewhere
She's coming
She does not know what waits in here

[Chorus]
I reached for dark and found a thread of white
My hand went out and something blazing came to light
I did not know what I was holding
I did not know it would be bright
But everything that moved toward me scattered
And I stood there in the wreckage of my own new sight
Corridors and spirals — I am learning on my feet
Finding what I carry as I clear the path between

[Bridge]
There are things in here she will not recognize
Things that do not die the way that living things die
Bone and chain and barb and something older
Things that answer to a name above the sky
I know this tower — I have been its prisoner
I know the floors she has not seen yet coming in
I have to reach the bottom before she does
She cannot face what waits here without me standing in

[Final Chorus]
I reached for dark and found a thread of white
And then I reached for both and kept them in one fight
I do not know the limits of this yet
But I am learning fast and moving by the light
Corridors and spirals — I am clearing what I can
She is coming and I will not let her face this unprepared

[Outro]
Every corridor is shorter than the last
Every spiral brings me closer to the west
I feel her at the threshold
I am almost there
This time I stand between her and the rest
