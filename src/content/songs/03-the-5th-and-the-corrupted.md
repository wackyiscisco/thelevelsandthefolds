[Verse 1 — low, controlled, they're hiding]
The texts were where the priest said they would be
Tucked inside a church that God forgot to free
We moved in quiet, we moved in fast
Gathered what we came for, didn't ask

Then outside — movement
The archer raised a hand
We pressed against the stone and tried to understand

[Pre-Chorus]
She came from the east
Like a tide that owns the ground
Purple and thunder
And not a single sound

[Chorus — tense, orchestral strings, no drums yet]
The 5th and the Corrupted
Riding through the south like she was born from it
The 5th and the Corrupted
And every soul behind her dragged into the myth
We held our breath
We held our ground
We watched her pass
We didn't make a sound

I watched the column stretch behind her throne
A hundred faces emptied to the bone
Blank eyes and bound hands, a broken row
And then —

One face that didn't fit the shadow

He looked ahead, not down
Not broken — not yet gone
Something still burning
Something still holding on
He had his brother's eyes
I could tell by how he kept them steady
Even in chains
Even in the dark
He wasn't ready
