[Intro — the tower, the star above, the silence after the claiming]
He has not spoken
In three hundred thousand years
He has only watched

And he speaks

[Verse 1 — the naming]
He says:

You have seen everything now

You have seen the machine
that built you without asking
You have seen the woman
who carried the light wrong
because no one told her the right way

You have seen the city
and the lies it told about itself
You have seen the ordinary ones
who held the door open
because someone had to
and they decided it would be them

You have seen the battle
You have seen the order
You have seen the corrupted light
born from the last thing
someone holy
had left to give

You have seen everything

And you are still standing

[Pre-Chorus]
And now

I will tell you
what you are

[Chorus]
You are the daughter of Time and Fate
Not chosen
Not trained
Not built
Not aimed

The daughter

The one Time held a place for
The one Fate drew a line toward
Across three hundred thousand years
of everyone
trying to make this moment
possible

Daughter of Time and Fate
The name was always yours
Before the church
Before the order
Before the star
Before the war

Before any of this
began

You were already
the daughter

[Verse 2 — what the naming costs, what it carries]
She does not feel what she expected

She expected it to feel like power
She expected it to feel like armor

It feels like grief

For every name on the wall
that did not make it here

For the woman on the floor
who bent the light wrong
and paid for it
with everything

For the letter
that Adina read
in the dark
alone

For the Lighted
who walked south
for two hundred and ninety thousand years
and never stopped believing
in a door
they would not live to see open

She carries them here

She carries them in

That is what the daughter is

[Pre-Chorus 2]
Not the ending

The arrival

Of everything
they carried
to this point

[Chorus]
You are the daughter of Time and Fate
Not chosen
Not trained
Not built
Not aimed

The daughter

Before any of this
began

You were already
the daughter

[Bridge — Initium Finis allows the three to see each other for the first time]
And then he turns

And she sees him

Really sees him

Not the presence in the field
Not the shape in the cosmos
Not the one who snapped his fingers
and brought her here

Him

The one who has been watching
since before she was born
Since before her mother was born
Since before the name Mydaal
was given to a city
that would one day
be called the last bastion

He has been watching

And he is

smiling

And she understands

This is not approval

This is recognition

This is what it looks like
when something that has waited
three hundred thousand years
finally sees
what it was waiting for

arrive

[Pre-Chorus 3 — the shift]
And then

Something on the other side of the landing

She does not hear it

She feels it

[Final Chorus — quieter, landing on the breath before the turn]
You are the daughter of Time and Fate
And so is she

The one who carried the south
The one who carries the north

The one who watched
The one who was watched

The one who lost the light
The one who kept the star

Time gave one daughter
Fate gave the other

And three hundred thousand years
have been the space
between them

Closing

[Outro — the turn, the recognition, the beginning of the walk]
She turns

And the landing stretches out before her

And at the other end of it

Someone is standing

She cannot see her face yet

She can feel her the way she feels Polaris

The fixed point
The familiar weight
The thing that was always there
even when she did not know
what to call it

She knows

She has always known

Across every scar
Every battle
Every field
Every order
Every name

She knew there was someone
on the other side
of the same war

And now

The space between them
is only

this

And they begin
to walk
