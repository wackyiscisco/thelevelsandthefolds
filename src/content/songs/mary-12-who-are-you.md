TITLE NOTE — STRUCTURAL / FORESHADOWING:  
“Who Are You” foregrounds WHO as an explicit mystery variable in the album finale. It does not resolve Hansel, his father, the creature/event at the library, or any supernatural identity. Hansel’s “I can’t answer” remains deliberately ambiguous: unable, unwilling, or genuinely not knowing are all still OPEN.
