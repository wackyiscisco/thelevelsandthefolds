Day twelve of training

[Chorus]

Boots and camps II — still going

Boots and camps II — still making this work

Somehow somehow

Through ancient complaints

Every surface and every ruin

Every beast and every poison

Somehow somehow — still making this work

[AEVUM + QUADE]

Aevum had seen everything — she said so constantly

"This again" — about the training ground

"This again" — about Quade

"This again" — when Quade arrived

Quade had four arms

Each moved independently

And with full conviction

And toward the wrong things

First arm: hit the post — "This again"

Second arm: hit the bucket — "This again"

Third arm: hit Aevum — [pause] "...this again"

Quade: "I am so sorry—"

Fourth arm hit something — "This again"

She had not moved

She had seen it coming

She had been seeing it for a thousand years

[RANGOR + HISTORIA]

Rangor's beasts followed him — this was the gift

It was also currently Historia's problem

Historia was writing

The beasts were interested in the writing

One sat on the notes

One ate a corner of the page

Rangor apologized — the beasts did not understand apologizing

Historia was now writing about the apology

The beast interrupted the apology entry

Historia wrote about the interruption

The beast interrupted that too

"I am writing a document about itself"

Rangor: "I am deeply sorry"

Historia wrote that down — the beast sat on it

[CASTOR + DISORDER]

Disorder agreed with everything Chaos said

Chaos was not here

Disorder began agreeing with Castor instead

"The formation is crescent" — "Agreed"

Castor said the opposite to test it

"The formation is not crescent" — "Agreed"

"These cannot both be correct" — "Agreed"

"You cannot agree with both" — "I agree"

Castor walked away

Disorder: "Agreed"

[HERC + TOXIN]

Toxin made poisons — she had never had a good test subject

Until Herc

"Try this one" — Herc tried it — "Are you affected" — "No"

Notes taken

"This one" — "Also no" — more notes — "Interesting. This one" — "No"

[long pause]

"How" — "I don't know"

"This is the most potent thing I have ever made"

Herc: "It tastes like grass"

Toxin: [writing very fast]

"This one will definitely—" — "Still no"

"You are incredible" — "Thank you"

"That was a scientific observation" — "I know. Thank you anyway"

[MADELA + RUIN]

Madela cleaned — Ruin ruined things

Not on purpose — that was important to clarify

Ruin could not specify which things — also important

Madela cleaned the table — it deteriorated

"Did you do that" — "I cannot specify"

"You are right next to it" — "I cannot specify"

She cleaned it again — it deteriorated again

"Ruin" — "I am sorry" — "Did you do it" — "I cannot specify"
