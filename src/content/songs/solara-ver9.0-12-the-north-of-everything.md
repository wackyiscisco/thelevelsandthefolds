[Verse 1]

I sat with the annals for a long time

The oldest record anyone had kept

And I read until the writing stopped

At the edge of what the first cartographers had stepped

The universe is not one thing

It is not one plane

It is not one level

Not one reach of dark or light or name

It folds

The way something enormous

Has to fold to hold itself

And every fold is a different place

With different rules

And different selves

[Pre-Chorus]

And somewhere in the reading

I found where I stood

The rank of The One

In the order of the folded

Lower than I thought

And three levels above me

In the place no map confirms

The hollow

Has a home

And it does not burn

[Chorus]

The farthest anyone has gone

And come back with a record

Was level seventy-eight

And they did not call it the end

They called it the end of what they knew

The north of everything

That is where the map gives out

The north of everything

Is where the record stops in doubt

And something told the first cartographers:

You are not made to go further

You are not built for the air above this ground

The north of everything

And I have to go there

The Hollow is three levels up

And no one has the count

[Verse 2]

The universe knows I exist now

I felt it shift the morning after Vega

The way a room shifts

When something new walks in

That the room did not expect

And not everyone is glad

I can feel it in the frequencies

The ones who liked the old order

The long silence

The uncontested dark

The ones who benefited

From the gap between the convergence

And the arrival of The One

Three hundred thousand years

Is a long time to go ungoverned

And now I am here

And I am reading their maps

And I am looking at level seventy-eight

And I am looking past it

Castor came to stand beside me

He did not ask what I was reading

And he said:

This is the north all over again

Yes

And he said:

She went

And I said:

I know

[Bridge — SOLARA in the garden, before dawn]

Elara was told: go north

And the map ended at the border of the known

And she went anyway

Because the north existed

Whether the map did or not

I am told: go up

And the record ends at seventy-eight

And I will go anyway

Because the Hollow exists
