[Intro — soft strings, lullaby warmth, just slightly wrong]
(barely heard, like a memory surfacing)
I knew you once...

[Verse 1 — intimate, almost gentle — she's describing a dream]
I keep seeing a child in the light
Younger than memory, older than reason
A warmth I have no name for in the night
That comes and goes with every passing season
I don't know her face — or I did once
Before the years made everything uncertain
She stood the way that sunlight stands at once
Like she was made before the world knew hurting

[Pre-Chorus — strings pull slightly wrong]
And I reach out
In every dream I reach
But there's always something pulling her
Just out of reach

[Chorus — soft but haunted, fragments of choir like echoes in a hallway]
Was she real
Or was she only something I invented
Was she real
Or have I spent a lifetime chasing something I imagined
I remember warmth
The way you remember something you were never told
I remember warmth
(barely, almost spoken)
And I have been so cold

[Verse 2 — the church connection forms]
I thought I found it once inside those walls
In the candlelight and incense and the singing
I thought I recognized it in those halls
That feeling, that specific kind of beginning
So I stayed — I gave it everything I had
I thought the warmth I felt was what they offered
But the warmth was mine — I brought it with me
(pause)
I had been carrying it since I was small

[Pre-Chorus 2 — quieter, more confused]
And now the walls are gone
The candles have gone cold
And I am back to searching
For something I have never fully known

[Chorus 2 — more desperate, choir fragments thinner]
Was she real
Or only something I invented
Was she real
The child I keep seeing in the light
I remember warmth
A hand, a face, a morning I can't locate
I remember warmth
(lower)
I don't know where I lost it

[Bridge — fever dream. Warmth and darkness alternating. The bass drone louder now.]
(spoken, half-dreaming)
There was a morning once. I was very small.
Someone was there. Someone full of light.
Not candle-light. Not altar-light.
Something older.
(the warmth flickers in the strings — then the drone swells slightly)
And then they were gone.
And I have been — looking —
(pause)
I don't know what I've been looking for.
I thought it was God.
(the drone settles, steady, warm in its own wrong way)
But it was never God, was it.
(barely)
It was something smaller than that.
Something that had my face.
-
-
[Final Chorus — choir gone. Voice + strings + drone. The darkness has warmth in it now.]
Was she real
I think she was real
Was she real
I think she is still out there somewhere
I remember warmth
Like something that was mine before I knew what mine meant
I remember warmth
(long hold)
And I am still looking
(the drone hums — not cold, not threatening — almost familiar)
(and that is the most terrifying thing)

[Outro — just the lullaby motif, one string, and the drone underneath]
(barely audible)
I knew you once.
(pause)
I think I would know you again.
(silence — then the drone holds one more beat before fading)
