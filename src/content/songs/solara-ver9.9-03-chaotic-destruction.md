[Pre-Chorus 3] Chaos stepped forward. Professional. Focused. Captainly. For nearly three seconds.

[Cancer:] Please tell me that’s water.

[Chaos:] Better.

[Cancer:] There is nothing better than water.

[Chaos:] Soup.

Longer silence. The kind that costs something.
