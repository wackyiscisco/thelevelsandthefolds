[Verse 1]
I walked these stones a thousand times before
When every crack and column held a name
The candles knew my footsteps at the door
The faithful sang and everything was flame

I heard the word carried on the wind from the east
Something dark and patient slowly rising
They brought it to the altar, brought it to the feast
And I said — I will go, I will hold the line

[Pre-Chorus]
(choir begins, distant)
They placed the mantle on my shoulders
They raised the flame above the nave
They said the darkness had been waiting
And I was everything they'd prayed

[Chorus — full choir, sacred hymn swell]
They called me The First Light
They raised their hands to heaven in my name
They called me The First Light
And every voice was certain I would claim the fight
They called me The First Light
And I believed them
Every word

---
[Verse 2 — stripped back, strings only]
Now only shadows fill the arching halls
The hymns I memorized have lost their sound
The stone still holds the cold of everything
But not the warmth of all the hope I found

I was so sure — I had studied every scar
Every dark thing that had ever risen
I had prayed at every altar, every dawn, every star
I said — I am ready, I was made for this

[Pre-Chorus 2]
(choir barely there, fading)
They placed the mantle on my shoulders
They raised the flame above the nave
They said the darkness had been waiting
And I walked out — unafraid

[Chorus 2 — same but beginning to hollow]
They called me The First Light
They raised their hands to heaven in my name
They called me The First Light
And every voice was certain I would claim the fight
They called me The First Light
And I believed it
Every word

---
[Bridge — piano alone, her voice barely above a whisper]
Now the halls don't answer
The altar holds no fire
The voices that once named me
Are lost inside the choir

A world that used to watch me
Has closed its eyes and gone
And I have paid in full
For every road I walked along

(long pause)

Now what.

---
[Final Chorus — choir returns, thinner, one voice gone]
They called me The First Light
They raised their hands to heaven in my name
They called me The First Light
The fight has taken everything I thought I knew
They called me The First Light

(the choir drops, just her)

And the first light always fades before the dawn

(silence)

---
[Outro — barely audible, voice in an empty room]
The halls remember
The stones remember
But the hands that held me up

(nothing)
