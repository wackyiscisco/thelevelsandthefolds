[Verse 1]
The gate was right there
Forty meters, maybe less

Castor said: steady
Castor said: keep moving
Castor said: we are almost—

The sound came first

The sound like something between
A warning and an announcement
Like something very organized
Had decided to have opinions about them

[Pre-Chorus]
The bees of Level thirty-four
Had lived here since before the chariot fell

They had built the guard
They had built the amber walls
They had built a queen
Who had been protecting this level
For a very long time

And four people had just walked into it

[Chorus]
The Hero and The Hunter
This is their kind of thing
The Hero and The Hunter
The bees picked the wrong four
Castor held the center
The Swan held something else entirely
The Hero and The Hunter
Figured out the weakness
Watch — watch —
This is what they do

[Verse 2]
Castor called positions
The Hunter read the swarm
There was a pattern — there is always a pattern
The Hunter found it in forty seconds
Which the Hero said was too slow

The Hunter said: you find the pattern then
The Hero said: I wasn't finding the pattern I was finding the solution
The Hunter said: what solution
The Hero reached into his pack

The Hunter said: why do you have honey
The Hero said: I always have honey

[Pre-Chorus 2]
The Swan started dancing

Not a fighting stance
Not a defensive posture
Just dancing
The way he always danced
Except now there was something else in it

Something that hummed like a song
That had been waiting in Level thirty-four
For a very long time

The bees nearest to him stopped
And watched
Because even bees know when something is moving
The way music moves

[Bridge]
The Hunter took the honey

He pulled back, he swung
He sent three jars of honey
In a clean arc directly at the gate

And every bee in Level thirty-four
Made the same decision
At the same time
In the same direction

Castor said: NOW

[Final Chorus]
The Hero and The Hunter
Sprinting for the gate
The Swan still dancing
Through everything
Not a single sting
Not a single landing
And the gate was right there
And they all went through
And Level thirty-four went quiet

[Outro]
Castor said: what are your names

They both looked at him
They both looked at each other

The Hunter said: I don't know yours actually
The Hero said: I don't know yours either
Castor said: we'll figure it out later

The Swan was humming something
It sounded like a sun chariot
Falling very slowly
Toward something that would catch it
This time
