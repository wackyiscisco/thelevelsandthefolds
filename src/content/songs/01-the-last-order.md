[Intro - war drums, distant, building toward silence]
I gave the order once before I understood what orders cost

[Verse 1 - commander's voice, controlled]
I had stood in worse places than this
I had held the line when the line wanted to break
But something moved through the valley that morning
Something that didn't come to negotiate — it came to take

I saw them forming at the ridge
I counted what we had against what was coming down
I made the calculation every commander makes
Before the numbers stop being numbers and start being ground

[Pre-Chorus]
So I raised my voice above the drums
Above the fear, above the smoke, above the sound

[Chorus]
RUN
I said it once and then I said it louder
RUN
I threw my whole voice at the open sky
RUN
They turned and looked at me and then they faced it
And I understood — they'd already decided where to die

[Verse 2 - quieter, horror settling]
I watched the first one fall
Then the second, then the third, then all the rest
I watched the women stand where I had told them to flee
I watched the children reach for hands that couldn't protect

There is a silence that belongs to a field after
A quiet that no living person should have to know
I stood inside it
I waited for my turn
And nothing came
The blade that should have found me
Didn't show

[Pre-Chorus 2]
She moved through everything like weather
Like a verdict that had already been designed

[Chorus 2]
RUN
I said it and they didn't and they're gone now
RUN
The last useful word I had in me
RUN
And she stood before me in the ruin
And gave me the one thing I did not want — she set me free

[Bridge - barely holding]
My people had the honor of a choice
They chose to stand
They fell for something real
I am still standing here for nothing
Not by my own hand

You spared me like a mercy
Like you thought that you were kind

The only one behind

[Final Section - stripped, near spoken]
This is the last order I will ever give —
Do not spare me
Do not spare me
Do not spare me

I am the only sound left in this field
