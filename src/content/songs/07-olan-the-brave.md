[Intro]
There is a man in the castle
And the castle is going dark
And he is still there

[Verse 1]
He brings the water before she asks
He sets the fire before she shivers
He has learned the specific silence
that means she needs the room
and the specific silence
that means she needs a voice

He does not ask her to come back
He does not say what he watches her becoming
He just tends
the way you tend something
you refuse to call lost

The raven on her shoulder
has too many opinions about the drapes
The formal woman by the door
is writing something in the margins of her notes
The wolf is at the threshold
watching everything with one eye closed
The priest is in the corner
and has not said a word in seven days

None of them are leaving
None of them are leaving

[Pre-Chorus]
When the titles fell / He stayed
When the castle emptied / He stayed
When the last advisor / packed his papers and his principles
and walked into the morning / without looking back
He stayed

[Chorus]
Olan the Brave
Not for the battle / Not for the war
Not for the glory or the name above the door
Olan the Brave
For the woman in the tower / Going dark by degrees
Olan the Brave
For the staying / when the staying
is the hardest thing / anyone has ever been asked to be

[Verse 2]
I watch the raven pace the windowsill
and tell the wolf exactly what she thinks about it
The wolf says nothing / The raven says more

I watch the formal woman cross the room
and set a cup beside the bed / with both hands
as if delivering something precious
and then retreat / as if she was never there

I watch the priest / sit in his corner
looking at the door / and not through it

And I watch Olan / touch the edge of the purple cloth
folded on the table / and leave it
because she'll know it was moved
and she'll know he was there
and right now / that is the whole of what he can give

[Pre-Chorus 2]

[Bridge]
They were here first
Before they found me / They were here
Standing in a castle / watching a purple woman
go the only direction / the world had left her
And they stayed
