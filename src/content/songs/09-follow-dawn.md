(spoken, soft)
You were taught that certainty
was the same thing as truth.

It isn't.

[Verse 1]
When the walls come down
It feels like the world should end with them
Every borrowed answer disappears
And suddenly the silence sounds enormous again

You spent so long becoming
What somebody else needed to believe
That now the mirror feels unfamiliar
Without their hands shaping what you see

[Pre-Chorus]
But breaking isn't failure
And doubt is not the end
Sometimes losing every false direction
Is how the real path begins

[Chorus]
Follow dawn
When you can't recognize the road beneath you
Follow dawn
When every truth you've held dissolves away
You don't need all the answers
You don't need to know who you'll become
Just keep moving toward the light
One morning at a time

Follow dawn
Even slowly still counts

[Verse 2]
I know what it feels like
To mourn the person you thought you were
To carry all those shattered pieces
And wonder which ones still deserve to hurt

But you are not the lies they handed you
And you are not the silence they designed
You are the voice still searching through the ruins
For something honest enough to survive

[Pre-Chorus 2]
So breathe through the uncertainty
Let the old world fall apart
Some truths only reveal themselves
After the breaking of the heart

[Chorus]
Follow dawn
Even if your faith arrives in fragments
Follow dawn
Even if redemption feels far away
You don't have to rebuild yourself
All in a single night
The sun still rises slowly
And somehow
So can you

Follow dawn
The morning is patient

[Bridge — strings only, exposed vocal]
Maybe this is what freedom sounds like

Not certainty
Not victory
Not peace

Just the terrifying silence
After someone else stops choosing who you are

And maybe now
for the very first time

Your life belongs
to you

[Final Chorus — key lift]
Follow dawn
Even if your hands are trembling toward it
Follow dawn
Even if the dark still feels like home sometimes
Every wall that disappeared
Made room for something real at last
And the girl you haven't met yet

May become the person
Who finally saves you

Follow dawn
You are not lost forever

[Outro — single piano notes]
The old story ended.

Good.

Now write
one that belongs to you.
