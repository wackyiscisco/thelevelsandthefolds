### Verse 1
Every word I ever spoke would bend time and space
Every step I ever took was met with bows and praise
Every gaze would turn toward me, every voice would fall in line
There was always something waiting, always answering in time

### Pre-Chorus
It was never questioned, it was understood
I was never questioned, nobody misunderstood
But the silence --

### Chorus
But the silence doesn't worship, the silence doesn't pretend
There is nothing left before me that answers in the end
No eyes to look upon it, no voice to call it true
The silence doesn't worship, it doesn't answer you

### Verse 2
The throne remains beneath me, the crown still holds its place
But neither one reflects me, neither speaks my name in grace
There is no reaction, no shift within the air
It is not defiance — there is simply nothing there

### Pre-Chorus 2
This was never how it worked, this was never mine
From everything to everyone to nothing and no one
There is silence now --

### Chorus
The silence doesn't worship, the silence doesn't move
There is nothing left to follow, nothing left to prove
No presence left to witness, no force to break in two
The silence doesn't worship, it does not answer you

### Bridge
If nothing kneels before me, if nothing turns to face
Was power ever present, or just reflected back in place?
It was never --

### Final Section
It was never just possession, it was never just command
It required something standing, something that could stand
But now it --

### Outro
And now it doesn't.
I am here with the silence that does not worship...
