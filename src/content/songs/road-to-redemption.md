[Verse 1 - piano alone, stripped, she's standing in a field with no one]
I am standing in a field with no one watching
No followers, no banners, no one's name
No order at my back, no voice to witness
Just the weight of who I was and what I'll claim

I've heard about the oaths that armies take
The raised hand, the promise, the drawn blade
But I have nothing like that — just this road
And everything I owe for what I've made

[Pre-Chorus - the call]
But the weak don't have an army either
The broken don't announce when they fall
The ordinary never make the record
And the forgotten — they get no one at all

[Chorus - the oath, full and clear]
So I swear it on this road
To the ones who have no voice
To the weak, the meager, the ordinary
The forgotten and the broken — by choice
I will come
Even if it costs me everything I have
Even if it costs me this life
I will come
This is my road to redemption
And I walk it alone tonight

But there's no one standing here to stop me
No council and no crown to grant me leave
I am the only witness to this moment
And I am enough — I have to believe
