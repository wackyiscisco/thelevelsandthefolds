Lost and Found

[Soft rain + distant orphanage sounds]

[Verse 1]

Eight years old, carrying buckets through the yard

Orphan kids marching like little soldiers, working hard

The grown-ups said, “Fetch the water, don’t make a sound”

No questions, no games, just keep your head down

But then I saw it — past the fence, in the junkyard glow

Old machines being smashed, thrown out like they didn’t know

Screens cracked open, wires torn from their chest

Something in my chest tightened… I don’t know why, but it felt like a test

[Pre-Chorus]

No phones, no screens, no lights that shine

Only one old radio in the study hall line

We’d gather every evening, listen to songs from far away

I never understood why tech was kept away

[Chorus]

Lost and Found

I didn’t know what I was looking at

Lost and Found

But something inside me remembered that

A broken thing, thrown away like trash

Why did it hurt so much to watch it crash?

Lost and Found

I think I was lost… before I was found

[Verse 2]

They said tech was dangerous, tech would make us weak

“Stick to your chores, boys — that’s all you need”

But every smashed machine felt like a piece of me

Like something important was dying quietly

I hid behind the fence, watched the metal bend and break

A roboduster tumbled out — old, dusty, maybe broken

I don’t know why, but I reached through the wire

Picked it up and hid it… my secret, my fire

[Pre-Chorus 2]

No one could know, no one could see

But that broken thing… it felt like family

I didn’t have a name for what I felt that day

Just a quiet promise I would keep it safe

[Chorus]

Lost and Found

Lost and Found

A broken thing, thrown away like trash

Why did it hurt so much to watch it crash?

Lost and Found

I carried it home that night, hidden under my shirt

Didn’t know what it was… but it didn’t hurt.
