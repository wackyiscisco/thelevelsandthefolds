[Verse 1]
Before they lined the roads when I returned
They'd lift their children up and call my name
Now I see them scatter, see the torches turned
The same hands that once held me pull away

I open my mouth — the words don't carry weight
I had a station once, I had their trust
But the dead I couldn't save are at the gate
And all the light I carried turned to dust

[Pre-Chorus]
(the questions, almost internal, barely sung)
Do I fight? And if I fight — for what?
Do I find the ones who might remain?
Do I walk until the world runs out?
Do I speak when no one speaks my name?

[Chorus — choir ghostly, buried low]
I am Commander of One
No standard left to raise, no rank to claim
Commander of One
Every road I walk, they fear the one who came
Commander of One
I used to bring them the sun
Now I only bring the cold

---
[Verse 2]
There was a girl who stood inside these halls
Who heard the hymn and answered — I am here
Who swore on every stone along these walls
To be the shield between you and your fear

Now those same stones are watching, cold and still
For the warmth I can no longer find inside
I reach for it — I reach — but reach until
The light I thought was steady starts to die

[Pre-Chorus 2]
(quieter, a confession)
Can I fight? The world is already ash
Is there anyone left to find?
Do I carry what I no longer have?
Or admit what I have left behind?

[Chorus 2 — same, heavier]
I am Commander of One
No standard left to raise, no rank to claim
Commander of One
Every road I walk, they fear the one who came
Commander of One
I used to bring them the sun
Now I only bring the cold

---
[Bridge — near silence, one cello, her voice alone]
I feel it going
That thing they named the light
I reach inside for what I was
And come back empty every time

They used to call me First Light
Now even I have stopped believing it
I am standing in the cold I made

(pause)

And I am all that's left of me

---
[Final Chorus — choir returns, then drops]
I am Commander of One
No banner, no faithful, no ground left to claim
Commander of One
They scatter when they hear me coming, fear my name
Commander of One

(choir cuts — just her)

And the Light of None
