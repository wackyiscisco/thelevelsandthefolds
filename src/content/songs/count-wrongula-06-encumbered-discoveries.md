**[Intro]**

Morning came
But not the same
The road was waiting
With Grandma's name

One old key
One hidden place
One quiet map
I had to trace

**[Verse 1]**

The morning after burial
I drove beyond the town
Past roads that looked forgotten
Where the fields grew wild and brown

The turns grew steep and narrower
The trees leaned close and still
Then suddenly it stood there
Like a secret on the hill

A mansion dressed in shadow
Five stories tall and worn
No signs of someone living there
No sound of anything warm

The windows held their silence
The gate had long since rusted
The whole place felt abandoned

And somehow still entrusted

**[Pre-Chorus]**

I held the keys
I held my breath
I held a thousand questions
On the edge of something left

If this was hers
Then who was she
And why had none of this
Been told to me?

**[Chorus]**

Encumbered discoveries
Heavy in my hands
Every room a question
Every answer changing plans

Encumbered discoveries
Dust and locks and light
A house that should be empty
Still watching me tonight

I came here looking for a past
I thought was mine to know

But every door I open
Makes the hidden deeper grow

**[Verse 2]**

I walked the halls in silence
Every landing, every floor
Five stories full of secrets
And each one asking more

Bedrooms dressed in stillness
Portraits turned away
Furniture with linen ghosts
That time forgot to claim

A grand staircase in twilight
A dining room gone cold
A music room with broken glass
And stories never told

Then down below the basement
Where the air grew strange and still
A single door stood waiting there

Shut against the farthest wall

I tried the keys in order
Each one failed the same
No metal turned
No hinge gave way
No plaque revealed a name

**[Pre-Chorus]**

So I went back up
Following dust and dim
Till I found the library wing

And something in me changed with it

**[Chorus]**

Encumbered discoveries
Shelves older than the paint
Books that looked like diaries
Preserved like acts of faith

Encumbered discoveries
Bindings cracked with age
Names and signs and creatures
Spilling from each page

Some I knew from lectures
Some I'd never seen before

And all of them were waiting
Like they knew what I came for

**[Bridge]**

There were stories filled with monsters
Some familiar, some unknown
Some in ink I could decipher
Some in languages I don't

One insignia kept returning
Stamped in gold and fading blue
Different names beneath the symbol
But somehow all of them rang true

**Ordo Custodum Velaminis**

Another read

**La Orden de los Custodios del Velo**

And one in English, clear as warning
Plain enough for me to read

**The Order of the Veilguards**

I said it softly to the room
Like maybe it would answer me

It didn't

But the silence changed

**[Verse 3]**

Then tucked between the journals
I found an old photograph
My grandmother much younger
Before the years I had

No softer silver patience
No gentle cardigan and cane
But fierce and bright and standing tall
Still carrying my face

There were twelve of them behind her
Lined in weather, lined in will
And there upon the left side
Made the whole house suddenly still

A little girl with dolls beside her
Maybe six or close to that
Red hair like mine in smaller strands

My mother

Laughing at the camera

I smiled before I knew I had
Like finding warmth inside the ache
But who were all those people
And what had Grandma tried to save?

**[Final Chorus]**

Encumbered discoveries
Heavier than before
Not just books and hidden rooms
But blood behind a door

Encumbered discoveries
A picture in my hand
A past that started breathing
In a way I hadn't planned

The house was dark
The hall was long
The silence almost kind

Then somewhere in the corridor
I heard a step behind

**[Outro]**

I turned too fast
The photo shook
The sound was closer now

Another step
Then one more still
Enough to end the how

A voice came through the shadow
Low and calm and strange

**"You finally came."**
