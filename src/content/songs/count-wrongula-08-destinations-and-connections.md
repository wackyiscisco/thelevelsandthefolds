**[Intro]**

Footsteps fade
The hallway clears
One old voice
Through fifty years

A stranger waits
But knows this place
And somehow knows
My grandmother's face

**[Verse 1]**

He looked older than the house felt old
Thin white hair and fingers cold
A cane beside him, shoulders worn
Like he'd been tired before I was born

But his eyes were bright
Alive and clear
Nothing faded
Nothing disappeared

Sit, child, please
No need to stand

His voice moved slowly
Like an hourglass in hand

Hansel

That was all he gave
Then looked around the room

I was a friend of Mary

And suddenly
Grandma had another name in someone else's memory

**[Pre-Chorus]**

Mary

I hadn't heard her called that way
Not with history behind the name

Not Grandma
Not the woman who raised me

Someone else's Mary
From another time completely

**[Chorus]**

Destinations and connections
Roads that crossed before my own
Lives that ran beside each other
Long before I had a home

Destinations and connections
Names I thought I understood
Every answer finds another road
Every road another wood

He knew her then
I know her now

Somewhere between us
There's a story somehow

**[Verse 2]**

He said they had been close once
Closer than most would know
But fifty years had gathered
Since he'd watched her go

There had been disagreement
He never called it more
Something neither one surrendered
Something left outside the door

She used to live here
Long before my time
Walked these halls
Called this estate mine

He heard she'd passed
He came too late
The funeral already gone

So he came here instead
To visit what remained

And found me standing where she'd once belonged

**[Pre-Chorus]**

I wanted to ask
What happened then
Why fifty years could separate friends

But something in his face
Made questions wait

Some stories need permission
Some answers need their date

**[Chorus]**

Destinations and connections
Paths that split and disappear
Some return after a lifetime
Some arrive too late to hear

Destinations and connections
People bound by things unsaid
Every photograph has someone
Standing just beyond the edge

He knew her young
I knew her old

And both of us
Were missing what she never told

Then Hansel reached inside his coat
And placed a key into my hand

Old metal
Dark with age
Heavier than something small should weigh

She gave me this a long time ago
He said it had been mine to keep
I tried returning it for years

But Mary always sent it back to me

Now she cannot

So you must

He closed my fingers over it

There were eleven keys

He looked toward the floor below

One of them opens that door

**[Final Chorus]**

Destinations and connections
One old key, one hidden way
Eleven pieces of a story
Scattered somewhere far away

Destinations and connections
Every ending leaves a thread
Mary's gone but all around me
Something keeps moving ahead

I hold the key
He takes his cane

The house feels different
After hearing Grandma's name

Hansel reaches the doorway
Then turns once more

Take care now

And nothing more

He walks away
Slow down the hall

One old man
One ancient key

Eleven keys

One locked door

And suddenly
Tomorrow feels very far from ordinary
