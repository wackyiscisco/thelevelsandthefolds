### Verse 1
Not a form I could define
Not a shape that would remain
Something standing in the fracture
There, just beyond the frame
It did not move toward me and it did not turn away
It stood within the ending but refused to fade
What are you?

### Pre-Chorus
There was no resistance in it, no demand to be seen
It's blinding, haunting, like a distant memory
Who are you?

### Chorus
A blinding silhouette just at the edge of sight
A shape I cannot follow, a form I cannot write
It does not answer to me, it does not align
A blinding silhouette that should not remain in mine
How?

### Verse 2
It was there when it should vanish
It remained when all was done
Every outcome reached completion — all except for one
There is no place to hold it
There is no meaning I can give
It exists without permission and continues still to live
Do I know you?

### Pre-Chorus
This was never part of it, this was never mine
Do you know not who you face at this time
Are you real?

### Chorus
A blinding silhouette, unmoved by what I claim
A presence without structure, without a name
It does not bend beneath me, it does not divide
A blinding silhouette still standing inside
Why?

### Bridge
I removed every obstruction, cleared every line
So why does something linger outside of design?

### Final Section
It does not break or answer, it does not resolve or fall
It does not reflect the ending — it does not end at all

### Outro
A blinding Silhouette
With a touch of red
Lingering by my bed
---I saw it clearly — and then it was gone.
