The road ends at the gate of the East
At the castle of ANU in ancient stone
He was standing there — familiar at least
The face she knew — but the face alone

(Priest): He's been here since before us
(Pride, stepping forward): You finally came. They said you would.

[Pre-Chorus]
The disguise held for longer than it should
He wore the dead and no one understood

Pride of the Seven
He was never the knight

Pride of the Seven
Drops the face in the light
At the gate of ANU — the first to arrive
He was never on their side
He was Pride

(Pride, barely): Not even from truth

(Archer, firm): I'll hold him.

She didn't wait for permission
She didn't wait for a plan
She stepped between Pride and the door
And she said — go — now — RUN

(Elara, in tears): ARCHER —

The silence on the other side too clean

(Elara, breaking): She said remember —

(Pride, cold and clear): She has entered the ground.
