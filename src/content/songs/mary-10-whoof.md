TITLE NOTE — STRUCTURAL / FORESHADOWING:  
Surface/title function: “Whoof” echoes an animal/dog sound while Mary is trying to identify the thing in the dark.  
W-system function: WHO becomes explicit as Mary’s question shifts from “what was that?” to “who was that?”  
Mystery rule: the animal-like / fur-covered sighting and Hansel’s appearance after the power returns are adjacent facts, not yet proof that they are the same thing.
