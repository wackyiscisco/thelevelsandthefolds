[Verse 1]
I saw him once in the tower
When everything he was poured through me
Not a face — just a weight
The kind that bends the world at the seams

I thought I would know him as power
As the thing that kept killing our names
But the edge of the universe finds him
Just an old man and the dark between the frames

[Pre-Chorus]
I do not speak
I sit beside him
He does not ask me why I came
I do not know what he was carrying
I only know it had a name

[Chorus]
Three hundred thousand years of holding
Three hundred thousand years of no
Every time they almost touched each other
He was the reason they couldn't go

What does it do to you — the waiting?
What does it do to you — the weight?
To carry the whole shape of the order
And watch the stars
And know you're too late

[Verse 2]
He looks at the stars like they are leaving
Like they owe him something they won't say
He looks at a tomorrow that might not come now
He looks at the light
That used to obey

He does not look like a villain
He does not look like what they warned
He looks like a man who held a burden
And forgot there was a time before

[Pre-Chorus 2]
I do not speak
He does not either
What would we say that could be true?
I feel everything he ever carried
I feel the version
He held for you

[Chorus]

[Bridge — spoken, no music]
Three hundred thousand years ago
Did someone sit with him?
Did anyone ask what it cost
To be the one who kept the world
From breaking open?
Did anyone say:
You were not born to this.
You were given it.
And you carried it.
And now it's done.

[Final Verse — her voice alone]
He is silent.
He is small.
He is looking at the stars.

Then he turns.
Then he looks at me.
And something in his face says:
I knew it would be you.

And he takes my hand.
And I let him.
And we sit.

[Outro]
At the edge of the universe.
Two hands.
One breath.
The weight of it all —
finally —
somewhere to rest.
