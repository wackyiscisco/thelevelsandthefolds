[Intro - sparse strings, large ruined space, she's waking]
Stone beneath my hands
A ceiling I remember from before
The smell of wood and ash
A place I swore I'd never see once more

[Verse 1 - recognition, slow and disoriented]
This is the church
The one they kept me in
Before the purple and the dark and everything
The walls are cracked now
Half the roof is gone
And through the rubble — light
The kind that doesn't belong

[Pre-Chorus - the nun appears]
And then a voice — soft, old, careful with each word
She says: I brought you here
I've waited to be heard

[Chorus - the revelation, ancient and celestial]
She says you are the light
I've always known you were the light
They locked me here for saying it
But child — you are the light
She says they built the dark around you
To keep you from the truth
And I have waited all these years
To say it to your face
You are the light

[Verse 2 - the offer, the refusal]
She reaches out her hand
Her knuckles like the ruins where she stood
I see the years of walls and silence
Written in every line she could

She says: let me help you
Let me walk this road beside
And I look at this woman
Who gave everything and still survived

I say: you've given enough
You're free now — that is yours
I will not take another person down with me
Before this war

[Pre-Chorus 2 - she accepts, quietly]
She doesn't argue
Just looks with eyes that have seen too much too long
And quietly — she lets me go

[Chorus]
She says you are the light
I've always known you were the light
They locked me here for saying it
But child — you are the light
She says they built the dark around you
To keep you from the truth
And I have waited all these years
To say it to your face
You are the light

[Bridge - the warning, voice changes, urgency]
Then she says — sharper now, afraid:
Child. Listen.
The girl in red is heading somewhere
She cannot come back from

She's already broken so much
And the world is cracking where she walks
You are the only one who understands
What she is walking toward

Go to her
Go to Elara
Stop her before she breaks what cannot be remade
You are the light she doesn't know she needs
Go —
Before it is too late

[Final Section - Pandora at the doorway, alone]
And I stand at the ruined threshold
And I feel the weight of everything she said
But I look at my hands
Still trembling
Still healing
Still carrying the dead

Not yet
Not now
Not yet

I'm not ready
She needs me whole
Not yet

[Outro - she steps out, the nun watching from the ruin]
The church behind me
The road ahead
She calls out softly
As I step into the light instead

Not yet
But soon
...soon
