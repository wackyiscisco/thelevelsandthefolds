[Intro — Pandora sees him through the fracture]
He is in the south
Far from the castle
Far from where the light went wrong
But the wrong light found him anyway
She can see it
Starting at the edges
The way corruption starts
Quiet
Then not

[Verse 1 — she crosses to him]
She does not know his name yet
She only knows what she sees
A man at the end of something
Kneeling in the wrong-colored dark

She knows this dark
She has been watching it spread
since the cradle fell

She reaches through the fracture
She does not ask permission
She puts both hands on what is wrong
and pulls

[Pre-Chorus]
And the light she carries
the clean violet light
the kind that was always hers
before it was anything else
does what it was made to do

[Chorus]
She heals him
Not because she has to
Not because she knows what he is yet
She heals him
because something in her
already does

And when he opens his eyes
He looks at her
And says
You survived

[Verse 2 — the reunion, what he sees]
He does not see Pandora
He sees Adonpra
He sees the face of the woman
who sent him away to live
who said go where I cannot follow
who gave him the only miracle
she had left

And she is here
She survived
She is here

And she lets him believe it
for a moment
because he has been carrying this
for longer than she has words for

And then she says
Not exactly

[Pre-Chorus 2]
She gives him only a few explanations
She does not have many left to give
She tells him what matters
and stops
before the grief
undoes the healing

[Chorus]
She heals him
Not because she has to
Not because she knows what he will carry
She heals him
because she knows now
what she is

And when she takes his hands
to cast the spell
he does not pull away

He has been waiting
to be found again
for a very long time

Across the years
Across the centuries
Across the distance between
now
and whenever she arrives

she says

when you feel something
that feels like this
Like her
Like the light
Like the moment someone puts both hands
on what is wrong in you
and pulls

He nods
He has always been hers

Then she opens the book
The Liber Primordius

She says:
Give this
to the next one
like me

And she means:
Give it back
next time we meet

[Final Chorus]
She heals him
She names him Keeper
She gives him the book to carry
She gives him a mission
to last three hundred thousand years

She heals him
Not because she has to
Because she already loves
what he will become

Because she already knows
how the story ends

Because she has already
received the book
from his hands

The way she came
Through the fracture

In the south
With the book
And the mission

when she feels like that again
