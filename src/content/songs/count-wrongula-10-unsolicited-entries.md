Hansel disappears
Before I find the words

And everything feels blurred

I look toward the hallway
Then down into my hand

What did you hide from me, Grandma?
What am I meant to understand?

The manuscripts and notes
Stack them in a cardboard box
Beside my coat

Things I'll take back with me
Things I need to read
Fragments of a woman
I thought had nothing left to keep

Then I remember
The basement door

Eleven keys
One lock
One reason to explore

Down five floors
Past dust and stone

Into the part of the house
That feels least like home

Trying to understand the rest

Old and wet and cold
Spiderwebs across the ceiling
Dust on everything I hold

Insects scatter underneath me
No footsteps marked the floor
No one has entered
In years or maybe more

I find a lamp
A box of matches

Starts surrendering its skin

Shelves upon shelves
Parchment and books
Photographs
Maps
And hand-drawn looks

Creatures sketched in charcoal
