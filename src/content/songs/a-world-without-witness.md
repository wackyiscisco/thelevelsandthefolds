[Intro — distant, almost hollow]
...No one saw it end.

### Verse 1
The final breath was taken
And no one marked the fall
Every name erased from time...
Unanswered, all
No eyes remained to follow
No voice remained to claim
What was lost
Or what became

### Pre-Chorus
I felt it close around me
The moment fully formed
Every outcome settled in...
Exactly as before

### Chorus — restrained, reflective
But no one saw it happen
No one saw me stand
No one left to carry it
No one left to understand
What does it become
When no one else remains?
What is victory...
Without a name?

### Verse 2
The crown still rests above me
The throne still holds its place
But meaning does not answer
In an empty space
There is no one left to measure
No scale to define
No one left to see the line
Between their end
And mine

### Pre-Chorus 2

Every thread undone

### Chorus
But no one saw it happen
No one saw me stand
No one left to carry it
No one left to understand
What does it become
When nothing else remains?
What is power...
Without a name?

### Bridge — deeper fracture, very minimal
If no one watched it end
Did it end at all?
If no one stood against me
Was there anything to fall?

### Final Section — almost spoken, quieter
This was meant to matter
This was meant to be
The final shape of everything
That answered back to me

### Outro — fading into void
...And no one saw it be.
