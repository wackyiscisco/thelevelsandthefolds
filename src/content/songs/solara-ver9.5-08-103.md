Level 103. Madela — ancient warrior turned gardener. Her plants keep dying. She receives word of the irregular (Solara). Calls her crew of 4 messy ancients. She cleans while they gear up. 'WE CLEAN.'

[Verse 1]

Level 103 — not a speck out of place

Every corner catalogued, every stone has its space

The dark does not drift here, the fold does not slip

Madela keeps order — she runs a tight ship

The garden is different

(plant seventeen — still dead)

The soil should work here

(plant eighteen — also dead)

Forty thousand years of trying

Every morning, same result

She plants

It dies

She cleans

[Pre-Chorus]

Then the signal came in

Irregular on forty

A The One who was not supposed to leave her system

Unsanctioned, unlogged, untidy

[Chorus]

We clean! we always clean!

Something slipped through the fold

We clean! we find the irregular

We file it, we fix it, we

CLEAN

[Verse 2]

She called the old crew — four ancients, still alive

Quade arrived first — knocked over six things just walking in

(four arms, you would think that helps — it does not help)

Rangor came next — seventeen beasts trailing behind him

She re-swept the floor before they finished sitting

Solvren opened a portal, something was cooking

(the good kind, today was a good day)

(last month he made something that ate through the wall)

Brome sat down and the chair was gone

Again

[Pre-Chorus 2]

She cleaned while they armored up

She organized while they argued about formation

She labeled every item in the room

While Quade knocked them off the shelf

One by one

[Chorus]

We clean! we always clean!

One The One out of bounds

We clean! forty levels of irregular

We pull it, we file it, we

CLEAN

[Bridge — Madela, flat and certain]

I was the finest warrior the dark ever raised

Then I retired

Then I found something better —

Purpose, order, the exact right place for every single thing

And that crew on level forty

Is extremely —

Messy

[Outro — march tempo]

Quade: ready! (three arms up — one is holding a vase she just polished)

Rangor: ready! (the beasts are already tracking mud back in)

Solvren: ready! (something is still on the fire — she locked the kitchen)

Brome: ready! (the door frame did not survive)

Madela: ...I'll sweep when we get back

WE CLEAN
