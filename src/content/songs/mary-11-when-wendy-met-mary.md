Musical direction: warm conversational skate-punk / indie-rock at first, playful bass and jangly guitars around Wendy/Karina banter; nostalgic strings enter during Wendy’s childhood memories; rain and darker guitars gradually overtake the arrangement on the walk to the library, ending in stunned near-silence.

**[Intro — café noise / cups set down]**

Two coffees.

Exactly enough.

No pastries.

Don’t ask.

Karina smiles.

“You always this cheerful, Wendy?”

Yes.

“Wow.”

Drink your coffee.

**[Verse 1]**  
She asks why I came to Central  
I say looking for a friend  
Maybe work if I get lucky  
Since employment had to end

“You got fired?”

Almost.

I quit first.

“That counts.”

No, it doesn’t.

“Kind of does.”

And you?

She stirs her coffee.

“My band fired me.”

...

Oh.

“Apparently I’m too weird.”

That I believe.

“Thanks.”

**[Pre-Chorus]**  
Two girls  
Two coffees  
Two spectacular career plans

One got dumped by coworkers  
One quit before they had the chance

Karina raises up her cup

“So we’re both jobless.”

Technically.

“Cheers.”

...

Fine.

Cheers.

**[Chorus]**  
Funny how a night can change  
Without announcing where it bends

Coffee with a total stranger  
Talking like reluctant friends

No great plan  
No grand design  
Just nowhere else we need to be

She asks where I’m going next

I say—

Someone I used to know  
Named Mary

**[Verse 2]**

“Tell me about her.”

Mary?

Childhood friend.

Haven’t seen her in ten years.

“Why?”

Family business collapsed.

Tailoring.

Good work too.

Didn’t matter.

Rent went up  
Money ran out

So we left Ridgefall.

I was young.

Mary was—

Mary.

Books everywhere.

Monster nonsense everywhere.

Karina freezes.

“Monster?”

Oh no.

“She likes monsters?”

Obsessed.

Her eyes light up.

Of course they do.

**[Pre-Chorus]**

“Monster nerd?”

Unfortunately.

“I love her already.”

You haven’t met her.

“Don’t need to.”

That is not how knowing people works.

“Where is she?”

At work.

“Let’s go.”

Why are you coming?

Karina looks offended.

“Monster nerd. Need I say more?”

...

Apparently not.

**[Chorus — brighter]**  
Funny how a night can change  
Without announcing where it bends

Ten years gone  
One old connection  
One brand-new irritating friend

I came here hoping Mary might  
Remember who I used to be

Now this girl I met three hours ago  
Is coming there with me

**[Verse 3 — walking / music starts darkening]**  
Dropped by Mary’s uncle’s shop  
Earlier today before the square

Asked if Mary still lived nearby  
He laughed and said, “She’s everywhere.”

School and books  
Then city library  
Late shift like she always did

Some things change  
Some things apparently

Stay exactly where they lived

Karina asks what Mary’s like

Smart.

Stubborn.

“Sounds familiar.”

No.

“Sure.”

**[Bridge — rain begins]**

First few drops.

Then twenty.

Then the entire sky gives up.

Karina pulls her jacket overhead.

“This city always like this?”

No.

**[THUNDER]**

Usually less dramatic.

**[Verse 4]**  
Library appears through rainfall  
Windows glowing down the street

Almost there  
Shoes completely soaked  
Cold water underneath my feet

Then—

**[POWER CUTS]**

Everything goes black.

...

Karina?

“Here.”

Great.

Lightning splits the sky above us.

For one second  
Library glass shines white

Then nothing.

**[Build — rain / low guitars]**

“Should we wait?”

No.

Mary’s inside.

At least—

she should be.

Then the lights come back.

One row.

Another.

Whole building humming alive.

Karina looks at me.

“Still wanna go in?”

Obviously.

**[Final Verse — doors open / music drains away]**

We cross the street  
Run through the rain  
Push the library doors apart

Mary—

There.

Near a table.

Red hair.

Same face.

Older.

But—

Mary.

And someone else.

A man.

On the table.

Clothes torn.

Blood on his hands.

Blood on his arms.

Blood—

What happened here?

**[Outro — almost silence]**

Mary turns.

Sees me.

Everything stops.

...

Mary?

Her eyes widen.

“Wendy?”

...

Ten years.

And somehow

that’s all either of us can say.
