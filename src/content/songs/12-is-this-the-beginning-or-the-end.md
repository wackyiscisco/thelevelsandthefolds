[Intro — stillness, faint organ, everything frozen]

No one moves.

Not her.
Not me.

And yet—
everything is waiting.

…

Is this the beginning…

…

or the end?

---
[Verse 1 — Elara arrives, grounded, observing]

I walked through what was left of it
Ash and fractured stone
Every place that fell behind me

Led me here alone

The walls are barely holding
The fire barely breathes
But something in this silence

Still refuses just to leave

And you stand there in the ruin
Like it never touched your name

Like everything that ended

Was exactly what you claimed

---
[Pre-Chorus — she studies Pandora, something feels off]

I should feel something break in me
Standing face to face

But something here is different

Something I can’t place

---
[Chorus — question reframed through Elara]

Is this the beginning
Or the end of what you’ve done
Is this the beginning

Or the last of everyone

You took the world down quietly
You let it disappear

But something still remains

…

Because I’m still here

---
[Verse 2 — recognition without understanding]

You look like every story
They were too afraid to name
Like something that was always there

Before it had a face

And standing here before you
There’s something pulling through

Not fear—
not surrender—

…

something almost like I knew

But I don’t remember how
And I don’t know what it means

Only that I’m standing

Where no one else has been

---
[Pre-Chorus 2 — tension rising inward]

You waited for me to come here
You built this final place

But you’re the first thing I have seen

That I cannot replace

---
[Chorus — stronger, more certain]

Is this the beginning
Of something you can’t control
Is this the ending

Of everything you stole

You say the world belongs to you
You say it’s all complete

But something still stands in front of you

…

And that is me

---
[Bridge — time suspended, realization without clarity]

The wind has stopped its movement
The sky forgot to fall
The dead don’t speak around us

There’s no one left to call

And yet this space between us
Feels heavier than war

Like something here existed

…

long before

(quiet)

I don’t know what I am to you

I don’t know what you see

But something in this moment says

You were not meant to finish me

---
[Final Chorus — calm, resolved, not victory]

Is this the beginning
Or the end we cannot name
Is this the beginning

Or the break inside your frame

You gathered every piece of this
You shaped it to your design

But something you could never take

…

was mine

Is this the beginning
…or the end

---
[Outro — cliffhanger, command hanging in the air]

You wanted me to come here.

…

I did.

Now we stand.

And everything is waiting.

(whispered, almost to herself)

If I say the word…

…they will follow.

(Pause)

If I don’t…

…this ends differently.

(Long silence)

Do I hold the line…

…

or do I give the order?

(Silence stretches)

I’m still here.
