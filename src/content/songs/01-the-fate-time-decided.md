[Intro — the battlefield, everything closing in]
She has nowhere left to go
The field behind her
The field in front of her
Everything coming at once
Everything at once

And then

A sound that is not a sound
A silence that snaps

[Verse 1 — from the battlefield to the cosmos]
One moment: smoke and iron
Everything that wants her gone
Every force that found a reason
Every tide she stood against

And then

A field

Not this field

A field that has no war in it
A field that has no edge
Where the sky is not a sky
but something older
that does not have a name
in any language she has spoken

And she is standing in it
And she is not alone

for a long time

from a place that watching
cannot reach
The one who made the rule
that says: I do not intervene

The fate Time decided

The fate Time decided
Was this

This field
This quiet
This proximity
The closest the daughters have ever been
without knowing it yet

The fate Time decided:
she would not fall today
Not today
Not here
Not before she knows

He does not say:
I am sorry it took this long
He does not say:
You should not have had to carry this alone

He says nothing

He raises his hand

He places it
very gently
on her head

And the knowing begins

Not like a wound
