Stone and empty air

No shift beneath the air

But now—...
Nothing answers anymore

If all things fall before me
If every voice is gone
Then what remains to follow...
What remains to respond?

### Final Section — softer, emptier
There is no resistance
There is no reply
There is no one left to witness...
There is no one left to deny

### Outro — fading
