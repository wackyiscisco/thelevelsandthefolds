[Verse 1 - violin + first harp notes, tentative, morning quiet]
I step outside where I've been hiding
The east is quiet, early, still
There's a road that runs along the valley
And it goes further than I will

But I can see them from the doorway
Scattered figures, scattered needs
The kind of tired that lives in people
Long before they start to bleed

[Pre-Chorus - the pull]
I could turn back inside
I could say it isn't mine
But something in me doesn't let me
Just walk past and leave it behind

[Chorus - soft but decided]
So I walk toward them
Not because I'm healed
Not because I have the answer
Or know the way to feel
I walk toward them
Because the east is rising
And something pulls me forward
Even if I don't know what that is

[Verse 2 - small acts, no fanfare]
There's a woman at the roadside
With a child she can't console
I sit with her, I don't explain myself
I just help and then I go

There's a man who's lost whatever faith he carried
I don't preach and I don't promise anything
I just stay until the trembling fades
And leave before he asks my name

[Pre-Chorus 2]
I don't say who I am
I don't explain what I was
Whatever I declared before
Stays behind me in the ash

[Chorus]
So I walk toward them
Not because I'm healed
Not because I have the answer
Or know the way to feel
I walk toward them
Because the east is rising
And something pulls me forward
Even if I don't know what that is

[Bridge - celestial choir enters, soft, barely there at first]
I've declared myself The Wicked
I've declared myself The Corrupt
But maybe — just maybe —
That was never all I was

And maybe all the darkness
That they planted in my chest
Gave me eyes for where it lives in others
And hands for what comes next

[Final Chorus - angelic hyms]
I walk toward them
Still learning how to heal
The east is rising
And for the first time — it's real
I walk toward them
I don't have a name for this yet
But it's pulling me forward
And I'm not looking back

[Outro - choir hum, alone but not lonely]
East rising
East rising
This is where it starts
