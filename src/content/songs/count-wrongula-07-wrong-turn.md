**[Rubato Intro]**

Three days riding
Polished black
Wheels keep rolling
No turning back

Forest waiting
Road unspools
Perfect weather
Questionable rules

**[Verse 1]**

Carriage wheels on the old stone track
Mountain fading slowly at our back
Fredo steady with the reins in hand
Like he could drive through fire and still look bland

Tisha's reading three books at once
Murmuring footnotes under her breath for fun
Bella watches trees with a narrowed stare
Watching me watching like she knows I don't care

I say the journey's perfectly tame
Bella says confidence and foolishness often share a name
Tisha keeps reading, Fredo keeps pace
And I keep wearing calm across my face

**[Pre-Chorus]**

Three days there
Three days home
One dark carriage
One stretch of road

Everything orderly
Everything neat

Until the forest decides
To interrupt my week

**[Chorus]**

Wrong turn
Wrong turn
The sign was subtle, I will admit
A broken pass and a bridge in bits

Wrong turn
Wrong turn
Bella says fate has a twisted sense
I say detours are not an offense

One road closes
Another appears
What could possibly happen here?

Wrong turn
Wrong turn

And still we press ahead

**[Verse 2]**

Then in the middle of the afternoon
The path runs dead beneath a shattered moon
Bridge gone missing, the ravine laid bare
Splintered boards and silence in the air

Fredo slows with elegant control
Tisha looks up from an annotated scroll
Bella steps down, scans the broken stone
The kind of damage saying we should leave it alone

Nothing about this feels right
Nothing about this feels planned
Nothing about broken timber
Says continue as intended

Bella says turn back while we still can
Tisha agrees with a small raised hand
Fredo says little, which is somehow worse
When the quiet one frowns, I begin to feel cursed

**[Pre-Chorus]**

A side road waits
Half lost in green
The sort of path polite maps never mean
Thin as a whisper
Dark as a thought

Exactly the kind of road
That says absolutely not

**[Chorus]**

Wrong turn
Wrong turn
Bridge out, warnings in the breeze
Three against one and they all say please

Wrong turn
Wrong turn
Bella says nothing good lives that way
I say nothing good ever comes from obeying decay

One road closes
Another appears
And my companions acquire opinions and fears

Wrong turn
Wrong turn

I tell them all it's fine

**[Bridge]**

Fredo turns the horses
Tisha shuts her books
Bella gives the side road
One long unimpressed look

Branches bend together
Like fingers overhead
The air grows cooler
The birdsong dead

Wheel by wheel
The road grows strange
Stone turns soft
And shapes all change

Trees lean nearer
Roots creep wide
The forest seems to shift
To see who's come inside

**[Theatrical Lift]**

The light goes dim without the sun going down
The leaves stop moving but still make a sound
The horses stiffen
The lantern swings

Something in the distance
Listens without wings

Tisha finally closes the last of her texts
Bella says this is exactly what she would expect
Fredo keeps driving, calm as before
Which somehow makes it all feel stranger than the forest floor

**[Final Chorus]**

Wrong turn
Wrong turn
The road less traveled now travels us
Every mile humming dangerous

Wrong turn
Wrong turn
The trees stand closer than they should
Like the whole world knows we're in its wood

No map mentions
A place like this
No wind carries
A hush like this

Something waking
Something stern
Watching closely

At our wrong turn

**[Vamp Outro]**

Bella rests one hand near her blade
Tisha grips the satchel where her old notes stay
Fredo guides the carriage through gathering shade
And I look ahead like this was all my idea

The forest deepens
The path grows thin
The dark itself seems to breathe us in

And somewhere beyond the crooked fern

Something smiles

At our wrong turn
