**[Rubato Intro]**

Night coming down
Forest closing in
One narrow road
One village within

Lanterns low
Roofs leaning tired
Smoke without supper
Eyes without fire

**[Verse 1]**

We came upon the village
Just before the light was gone
Broken fences, muddy pathways
Too many stomachs holding on

Coyolings gathered near the houses
Packs worn thin from going without
Cubs too tired to make a racket
Mothers with no milk to give out

Those who still could cross the distance
Brought back scraps when instinct came
Not enough for every household
Not enough to dull the pain

Markets sold what packs required
At prices few could ever meet
Hunger had become a business

And business had learned how to eat

**[Pre-Chorus]**

Bella watches every rooftop
Tisha takes the village in
Fredo holds the reins beside us
While the night grows dark and thin

Then an elder steps before me
Gray around the ears and eyes

Denta

Firm despite the hunger

Guarding what remains inside

**[Chorus]**

No invitation required
Not tonight
Not when hunger's at the doorway
And the cubs have lost the fight

No invitation required
Keep your gate and keep your pride
If the only rule between us
Leaves the starving ones outside

You can tell me turn around
You can tell me I don't belong

But hunger does not care
Who entered right or wrong

**[Verse 2]**

Denta says they need no visitors
No one enters after dark
No outsiders in the village
No exceptions past the mark

Before she reaches the ending
I have somewhere else to be

Smoke curls once around my shoulders

Then the road is missing me

I appear beside a young one
Small enough to barely stand
Bella groans from somewhere distant

I put bread into his hand

He turns it over cautiously
Sniffs the crust and looks at me
Never seen a thing like this before

Which feels absurd, admittedly

**[Pre-Chorus]**

One small bite
One widened stare
Another bite
Then crumbs everywhere

The cub looks up
And asks for more

And suddenly there are faces
At every door

**[Chorus]**

No invitation required
Not tonight
Not when someone doesn't know
What breakfast even tastes like

No invitation required
Call me rude and call me vain
But a boundary built around starvation
Is a boundary I'll profane

They can argue about custom
They can whisper that I'm wrong

The hungry do not need permission
To survive another dawn

Everything we packed

Fredo

Pastries too

Something gentle

Pastries

Still unsure of what she's seen

I smile
