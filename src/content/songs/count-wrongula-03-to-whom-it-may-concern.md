**[Rubato Intro]**

Afternoon tea
Western sun
Porcelain waiting
The day half done

One measured spoon
A civilized cup
Newspaper open
Let's see who's messed up

**[Verse 1]**

Front page misery, column number four
Feeding shortages hitting the poor
Market shelves thinning, traders complain
Sources constrained and the prices insane

Some travel freely wherever they choose
Some follow instincts they cannot refuse
Some never crossed, some never can
Some lost the instinct no one understands

Now traders are charging whatever they please
For marrow and morsels and species-specific needs
The wealthy still dine
The desperate wait

Funny how hunger
Can recognize one's estate

**[Pre-Chorus]**

Supply goes down
The numbers rise
Another neat excuse in bureaucratic disguise
Wrongula sighs
And turns the page

The world has apparently
Misplaced its brain

**[Chorus]**

To whom it may concern
Apparently nobody has a name
Stamp it, seal it, send it out
And call indifference a game

To whom it may concern
Such an elegant little phrase
Pass the problem desk to desk
While the starving learn to pay

Pour another cup
Surely someone will look up

Eventually

**[Verse 2]**

Soft steps enter, flour on a sleeve
Tisha from kitchens has something to leave
Young for a witch at two-twenty years
Still new enough to tolerate everyone here

Black heavy envelope
Thirteen seals red
Wrongula lowers
The news he has read

The Tridecagon of Old
Thirteen vampires enthroned
Eight hundred years deciding
What everyone should have known

He turns the letter
His eyebrow ascends

There on the front
The insult begins

**To Whom It May Concern**

His smile becomes terribly refined

**[Pre-Chorus]**

They know who I am
They know where I live
They know every title etiquette can give

And after eight centuries
This is their art?

Apparently manners
Have fallen apart

**[Chorus]**

To whom it may concern
Thirteen fossils dressed in lace
Eight hundred years in velvet chairs
And still they cannot address a face

To whom it may concern
What a beautifully polished slight
Invite a Count without the Count
Then summon him tonight

Seal it in red
Letter it gold
Nothing says respect
Like bureaucracy old

Concern has arrived

**[Bridge]**

He breaks the seals
Unfolds the page

An audience requested
Attendance expected
Formal dress preferred

Naturally

Not the summons
Not the hall
Not their ancient council call

Of every offense
Large and small

It's the greeting
Most of all

**[Final Chorus]**

To whom it may concern
Fine, consider me concerned
You called without the courtesy
But courtesy can be returned

Let them stare
Let them pry
Let them wonder what and why

Why I breakfast
Why I dine
Why the daylight suits me fine

If they wanted my attention
They have managed that at last
