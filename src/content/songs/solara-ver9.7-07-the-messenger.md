[Verse 1]

Nobody saw him coming

Mira would have warned them

She was three minutes behind

He came through the east gate

The same gate The Ones had come through

With their confetti and their drum

He had no confetti

He had a message

And not much else

[Pre-Chorus]

He fell at the threshold

Everyone stopped

Crotus moved first

Then Solvren

No order — no discussion

Just: soup

[Chorus]

The messenger came from 280

He was dying when he arrived

The messenger came from 280

The soup kept him alive

What he carries

Changes everything

The messenger from 280

Is alive

[The soup scene]

Crotus held him up

Solvren had the bowl ready

It was not planned — the soup was simply there

Because the soup was always there

Because Solvren cooked and Crotus carried

And that was how it had always been

He drank — once — twice

Thresh: "Current excellence percentage"

Solvren: "Today — eighty-one"

Thresh said nothing — he wrote it down very carefully

The messenger breathed

Then breathed again

Then kept breathing

Cassie: "That should not have worked"

Solvren: "It was a good batch"

Crotus: "It was a very good batch"

He breathed again

That was the miracle

And the miracle was soup

[Verse 2]

They waited

He looked at Solara

She was already in front

Because Helena had put her there

And Helena had been right to

"280," he said — "Yes," said Solara

"We lost it"

Silence

Not the quiet kind

The kind that costs something

"The darkness moved faster than we anticipated

We held as long as we could

We are at 279 now

And 279 will not hold much longer"

[Bridge]

"How long," said Cassie

"Days" — "Not weeks" — "Days"

One month had become days

Solara looked at Castor

Castor looked at the plan — the crescent formation

All of it still true

But weeks had just become days

Mira: "I should warn you—"

"Mira"

"I know. But I should warn you anyway"

"What"

"This is the moment the war begins"

Pause

"You are three minutes behind"

"I know," said Mira

"But I wanted to say it"

[Pre-Chorus 2]

Chaos had nothing wrong to say

He was quiet

That had never happened before

Void looked at the door the messenger had come through

As if calculating how many were behind him

He did not share the number

[Final Chorus]

The messenger came from 280

We have days — not weeks

The darkness is moving

We move first

What he carries changed everything

We don't march in one week

We march

[Outro]

Solara: "How is he"

Crotus: "Stable" — "The soup?" — "The soup"

She looked at Solvren

Solvren: "Eighty-one percent today"

"Good" — "It was a good day to need soup"

Solara turned to the room

The room turned to her

"Three days"

Nobody argued

The messenger was already asleep
