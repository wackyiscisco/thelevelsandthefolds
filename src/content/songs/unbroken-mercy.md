[Verse 1 - urgency, faster tempo, she's already moving]

The Order and the Riders closing in
And someone I've been keeping at a distance
Is leading people who have never fought before

They're not an army — they're a gathering
Of ordinary, broken, meager, forgot
The very ones I swore to on that road —
They're walking into something they cannot stop

[Pre-Chorus - no hesitation]
I don't think twice
I don't count the cost
I just turn west and run

[Chorus - she arrives, the sight hits her]
And there they are
A sea of white and grey and iron
The Order and the Riders, wall to wall
And there she is
In crimson at the center
The woman who once drove the blade that changed it all

The last of what I am and what remains
I don't know if it's enough
But I have no choice

[Pre-Chorus 2 - she reaches down]

Into everything that's left
