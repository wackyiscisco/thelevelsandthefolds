[Intro - atmospheric, no drums, low strings and distant choir]
I learned a word and it undid me
I burned my throne and walked into the world
And for the first time in my long remembering
I was looking for something instead of taking it

### Verse 1 - single drum enters slowly
I combed the mountain passes and the valleys choked with ash
I searched the ruins of every cathedral I had passed
I turned the shadows over twice, three times, a hundred more
For a face that matched the page I read, a face I'd seen before
I saw the remnants of my kingdom watching from the trees
I kept walking, kept my eyes down, kept the thread
The crimson thread that wouldn't stop, the pull I couldn't freeze
That feeling I had no name for — it was living in my chest instead

### Pre-Chorus 1
Not for conquest
Not for power
Not for everything I burned and built and bled for years
For the first time
In this body
I was chasing something fragile, something near

### Chorus 1
This is hope and it is desperate
This is love and it is strange
I have lived three hundred winters without wanting anything to change
But I am crossing every border, every river, every scar
Just to find the other half of what I am

### Verse 2 - drums drop, ominous quiet, single cello
I found the church I turned to cinders at the end of the last war
The stones were black, the altar cracked, I recognised my own score
And there amid the remnants of the thing I had destroyed
I heard a sound that split me open, something broken, something void
My faithful, my remaining, the ones who had not run
Had circled her like a trophy, like a prize they thought they'd won
They thought their queen would want this, thought they'd done a brilliant thing
They didn't know their queen had changed before they'd finished reckoning

### Pre-Chorus 2 - cold, descending
I saw her face
I saw the blood
I saw what my own hands had built and set loose in the world

### Chorus 2 - FULL EXPLOSION, everything crashes in at once
UNHAND HER
And they turned and looked at me like I was something they'd misread
UNHAND HER
And I swept them from the altar and I left them where they bled
Not for conquest
Not for power
This was something I had never learned to do — protect
I cleared the ruin
I cleared the silence
And the only two left standing in the wreck

### Interlude - total silence, single piano note, near a cappella
She looked at me like ruin
Like the end of everything she'd held
Her eyes were red and burning
Full of blood and grief and fell
I had rehearsed a thousand words
I had them all in line
But standing in the ash of what I'd burned
She erased every line

### Bridge - sparse, barely a melody, then building
She said YOU
And the word fell like a verdict on a wound
And I had nothing
Just her name inside a room

Elara

One word, barely breathing
And she answered with her blade

She drove it through before I finished
In the silence I had made
I heard the lightning before I hit the ground
I heard her running through the storm, not turning around

### Post-Bridge - Pandora on the ground, low orchestra swells
I am older than this wound
I am deeper than this scar
She could not have killed me even if she'd aimed straight at my heart
But I am lying in the ruins of the church I burned before
And the only name I've ever learned to love just left me on the floor

### Final Chorus - same words, completely destroyed
This is hope and it is desperate
This is love and it is strange
I have lived three hundred winters without wanting anything to change
And I would cross it all again, every river, every scar
Just to know the name of what I am

### Outro - extended, near silence, strings only, spoken
I see her in the distance
Crimson, not the dawn I thought
She is carrying everything I made her
She is everything I brought

Mercy, gone
She is running from the light
She is running from the name
She is running from the altar
Where we almost were the same

### Final lines - barely spoken, then silence
I found her
And she ran me through

Close your eyes now

This is what hope costs you
