[Intro - single held choral note, like a bell that won't stop ringing]
Victory has a silence.
Defeat has a sound.
And it is louder than anything I have ever known.

[Verse 1 - soft, reverent, each name its own weight]
There was a woman who would break her bread in two
Never asked if I was hungry, just already knew
She tasted every sunrise like it might be her last gift
I can still smell the warmth of it
I cannot find the lift

There were children with small hands and questions about war
Who told me I was sunlight when I walked through the door
They braided red into the morning every time I knelt
They called me — the Sun
They called me — the Sun
And I could not keep them warm

[Pre-Chorus - voice cracking, celestial hum beneath]
I hear them still
Not in silence — in the sound that fills the space they left
A sound more absolute than anything
That has ever measured loss or depth

[Chorus - soaring then falling, choir enters and breaks]
This is the sound of defeat
Not the drums, not the blade, not the burning
This is the sound of defeat
Every name I will never stop hearing
Every laugh that will not come again
Every door that will not open
Every morning they were taken from
This is what defeat sounds like when it enters you
And doesn't leave

[Verse 2 - intimate, almost too quiet to hear]
There was a knight with careful eyes who never said a word
But looked at me like maybe I was something to be heard
I never told him that I saw it — there was never time
He fell somewhere behind me
And I never looked behind

And my horse — oh my horse
She knew the way before I did
She knew the weight of what I carried
Every field, every ridge
She trusted every order that I gave her
Even the last
Even the last

[Pre-Chorus 2]
They marched because I asked them
They held because I held
And I am standing in what remains of all of that
And something cold has come to dwell

[Chorus 2]
This is the sound of defeat
Not the drums, not the blade, not the burning
This is the sound of defeat
Every footstep that will not come marching
Every voice that called me the Sun
Gone into a ground I couldn't harden
How did I not see what was coming
This is what defeat sounds like
When it becomes the question you cannot answer

[Bridge - the blame arriving]
I gave the order
I chose the ground
I read the field and I was wrong
I chose the stand
I should have fled
I should have taken everyone along

Was it pride
Was it certainty
Was it the thing I told myself was strength
That walked them into something
I could not judge the length

[Final Chorus - changed, darker, the hymns fracturing]
This is the sound of defeat
And it will not stop
And it will not stop
And it will not stop

The Sun they called me
The Sun
I could not keep a single one of them warm
This is the sound of defeat
And her name is
Elara

[Outro - a capella, celestial choir fading out]
The sound of defeat
Is your own name
Called by the ones
Who trusted you
And are no longer here
To call it
