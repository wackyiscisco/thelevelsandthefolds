[Intro — the lullaby motif surfaces, warm, fragile. Then the drone pulls it under.]
(beat of silence)
(warmth tries again — then goes quiet)

[Verse 1 — controlled, almost rational — she's convincing herself]
I know what I dreamed last night
I know the warmth I felt inside it
I know the shape it took inside the light
And I know better now than to confide it
It was grief — it was the aftermath
Of every wall I burned to get here
It was nothing but my mind retracing
Every road that once felt warm and clear

[Pre-Chorus — starts certain, ends less so]
I have been deceived before
By things that felt like answers
I have chased a feeling through a door
That only led to harder
So this one — I let go

[Chorus — resolute but wrong, the warmth strains underneath]
I will not follow a dream I cannot name
I will not chase a warmth I cannot claim
I have been fooled by beautiful before
I know the sinful act of wanting more
The sinful act of thinking something waits
The sinful act of opening closed gates
I will not do it — I will not be moved
(barely, under her breath)
I will not follow

[Verse 2 — the doubt creeping in despite herself]
But I keep seeing it — that same small light
The same familiar warmth that has no reason
I keep reaching in my sleep each night
For something that returns with every season
I have abandoned everything I built
I have walked away from altar, stone, and silence
And still this dream returns without my guilt
(pause)
Like it was always there before the violence

[Pre-Chorus 2 — the certainty cracking]
What if I have been looking
In every place but one
What if the warmth I'm chasing
Was never in the church or in the sun
What if it's — no.

[Chorus 2 — same words, different weight — she's talking herself back from the edge]
I will not follow a dream I cannot name
I will not chase a warmth I cannot claim
I have been fooled by beautiful before
I know the sinful act of wanting more
The sinful act of thinking something waits
The sinful act of opening closed gates
I will not do it — I will not be moved
(louder, desperate)
I will not follow

[Bridge — the lullaby motif pushes hardest here. The drone is loud. War between them.]
(spoken — exhausted, almost pleading with herself)
It is just a dream.
It is just a memory of something that never fully was.
You have been deceived before — by warmth, by light, by the idea of being found.
You walked away from the church for a reason.
You walked away from everything for a reason.
(the lullaby motif swells — almost breaks through)
(a long pause)
(the drone wins. The warmth fades.)
It is just a dream.
(quietly)
Let it go.
--
--
--
[Final Chorus — drone dominant now. The warmth is gone. She won. She lost.]
I will not follow a dream I cannot name
I will not chase a warmth I cannot claim
I chose this — I chose the open dark
I chose the silence over falling apart
The sinful act of denial is a mercy
The sinful act of denial keeps me standing
I will not follow
(held, final)
I will not follow

[Outro — drone alone. The lullaby motif is completely gone. Just the darkness breathing.]
(nothing)
(the drone holds)
(fades)
