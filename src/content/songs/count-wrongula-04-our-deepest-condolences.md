Call the chapel, choose the flowers, try

Names and dates and papers by the door

Grandma always made things seem so light
Even when the world refused to turn out right
Now every room feels larger than before
And every little sound comes through the floor

I make some coffee
Forget to drink
Sign another page
Try not to think

There are arrangements
There are calls

A massive house outside the edge of town
Hidden past a road that winds around

Gray sky hanging over cemetery stone
