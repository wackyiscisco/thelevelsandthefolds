[Intro — distorted guitar, frantic, chaotic pulse]

[Verse 1 — battle chaos, Lust hitting the camp]
The meeting broke at dusk
They scattered to their posts
By the time the fire came
Half the camp had turned to ghosts
Not dead — still breathing
Still moving through the dark
But the hands were wrong
And the eyes had lost their mark

[Pre-Chorus]
Something moved between them
Something wrapped in what they need
The kind of weapon
That doesn't break the door —
It opens up the creed

[Chorus — heavy, raw, full band]
Not rage  Not cold
This one gets inside
Not sword  Not fire
This one makes you choose a side
Not the ones you face
But the ones beside your door
The Lust in us
Is easier to feed than war

[Verse 2 — Elara and the First Fallen Knight, unaffected]
She held the line beside him
The Knight who still had kind eyes
Whatever moved through all the rest
Passed through them
Like it recognized
The ones already given
The ones who made their war a vow
Lust found no opening in them —
They had already answered how

[Pre-Chorus 2]
Then the Nun stepped forward
Where none had right to stand
Drew something from the silence
Like pulling thread from sand

[Chorus 2]
Not rage  Not cold
This one gets inside
Not sword  Not fire
This one makes you choose a side
Not the ones you face
But the ones beside your door
The Lust in us
Is easier to feed than war

[Bridge — stripped, sacred, the ritual]
She didn't speak
She didn't have to
Every soldier who had turned
Went still
One word
From somewhere old
And Lust moved somewhere else instead

But the cost —
She had always known the cost

(She falls.)

[Post-Bridge — near silence, The Nun falls]
She held her while she fell
No words for this
A woman who crossed a siege
To do what no one asked
What no one knew to ask

Lust offers the trade —
The Nun returned
For only her.

She refused.

And then she was gone.

[Outro]
The Nun's hand had opened.
A ring.
A folded note.

She had written that the name at the top
Was not the Order's.
Was not the church's.

She had worn something once
Around her neck
Long ago
In a different life

She had thrown it away.

Now it was this.

A ring. One of two she says.

Whatever that meant.
