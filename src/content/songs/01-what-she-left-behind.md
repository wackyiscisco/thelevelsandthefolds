[Verse 1 - quiet, almost alone]
Horse is ready at the gate
Letter in my hand, I'm about to go
Then something on the ground catches the light
A box — wrong, broken, scattered on the floor
I should go
I know I should go
My feet won't move

[Pre-chorus 1 - slow, deliberate]
One page
Then two
Then my hands
Won't stop

[Chorus 1 - methodical, each name landing like a stone]
The hand above the hand — I'd heard the name
Those four who rode — I knew some faces
The sins of capital — I'd fought beside them once
The Commandment Keeper
Who keeps them?
Who keeps them?

[Verse 2]
Page after page
This was not a diary
This was a map
She made this for someone
Someone who might follow
The White Woman — what did she know?

[Pre-chorus 2 - the recognition building]
I'd seen these names before
In orders, in reports, in whispers behind closed doors
I was always on the other side of that door
I was never meant to read the list

[Chorus 2 - the list grows, dread building]
The arched ones — I'd felt their shadow
The ones below — I'd heard the ground shake
The One — they always said The One
The One — protect the One
That was my mission
That was my whole mission
Protect —
The One.

[Bridge - almost spoken over heavy guitar swell]
I was their Shield
That's what they called me
Not warrior. Not champion.
Shield.
Keep the One untouched
The One above everything
I spent years guarding something
I was never allowed to name

And now I read the name
In ink
In a stranger's hand

I was not the protector
I was the weapon
Pointed at everything else

[Final Chorus - cold, no hesitation]
The hand above the hand — I understand it
Those four who rode — already done
The sins of capital — every face accounted for
The commandment keeper — I will find them

The arched ones — I have questions
The ones below — I heard the ground
And at the top of every page
Just a name

The One —

[Outro - whispered]
What she left behind
Something not meant for me to find
But now I have it in mind

...I plan.
