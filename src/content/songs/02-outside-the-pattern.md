I land in a world that is broken but breathing
No monsters, no mist, but the fracture is real

And then — the thoughts
Like a current beneath me
Not voices exactly, more pressure, more weight
From below something watches
from above something holds its breath
and the world in between is awake

They're shouting about a woman in purple
