[Intro — the sound of waking up, stone floor, total silence]
I am awake

[Verse 1]
I know this ceiling
I know the exact weight of the dark in this room
I know the way the cold comes in from the left
Not the right
Always the left

I know this place

I know this place because every wound I was ever given
Was given to me here
Every thing that was taken
Was taken in this room

I am in the north
I have always been in the north
I just did not know that was its name

[Pre-Chorus 1 — she tries her voice]
I opened my mouth
And nothing
I tried again
Nothing

The voice that I was learning to reach toward the light —
Gone
The shock took it
Or Lust took it
Or the room took it
Something took it

And I am here with no sound
And no map
Except the one I left behind

[Chorus — the realization]
The map I left behind
Is everything I knew
Everything I learned before they brought me here
Every name, every gate, every note, every clue
I left it where she'd find it
If she came looking

I left it so she'd know
And knowing — I hope she stops
I hope she reads my words and turns around
I hope she finds the edge of what I left behind
And decides that I am not worth what comes next

Please
Let the map be enough
Let it be enough to turn her back

[Verse 2 — she takes stock]
I have my hands
I have my eyes
I have everything inside my head
Which is where all of this will have to live from now on

No one can hear me
Which means they cannot stop me
With a word
Or a name
Or a warning

Silence is not nothing
I am learning that right now

I have been taken
But I have not been finished
And no one in this room
Knows the difference yet

[Pre-Chorus 2 — Elara]
She is out there
I know she is out there
She moves like a storm and the world notices

She found my notes and she is reading them
Right now
Somewhere out there in the west or the south or the gap between —
She is reading what I left
And I am asking the only person who cannot hear me —

Stop
Please stop
This is not a place for you
This is not your war

[Chorus 2]
The map I left behind
Was meant to end the search
Every note I wrote was pointing somewhere else
Somewhere far from here, somewhere that hurts less
I left it like a door I hoped she'd close behind her
I left it like a last word
I left it like — please let this be enough
Please let her read it and decide
That I am not worth what this door opens into

[Bridge — she stands up]
But she won't
I know she won't
She is Elara

So I will have to be out of here
Before she arrives
No voice
No map
No record of what this room has done

Just the door
And whatever is on the other side of it
And me
Going through it
In silence

[Outro — internal, resolve forming]
I will not call for her
She cannot hear me anyway

I will not leave a trail
I will not leave a mark
I will not give this place one more thing to use against me

I left the map behind
I am not leaving anything else

I am going to get out of here myself
And when she arrives at the door I came through —

I hope it is empty
I hope she finds nothing
I hope she turns around

But if she doesn't —

At least I will have tried
