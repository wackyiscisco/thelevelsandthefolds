[Verse 1]

They arrive without announcement

Seven figures at the garden's edge

Not the way that power moves through doorways

More like light that settles on a ledge

I know them before they speak their names

I carry every version of the world they kept

And they look at me the way the tired look

At the one who finally woke while they still slept

[Pre-Chorus]

They are not afraid

They are not grieving

They came in peace with nothing left to prove

Something in their faces says:

We waited for this

We just wanted to see you before we moved

[Chorus]

Remnants of the past

Standing in my garden

Humble, kind, and patient at the door

Everything they carried

Now belongs to no one

The old names cannot hold the world anymore

Remnants of the past

Content to be departing

They came to say the thing they needed to say

I am what they built for

I am what they stayed for

And now — they're going to go away

[Verse 2]

Humility bows first — she does not speak

She only lowers her head and steps aside

Charity takes both her hands and gives them

Then turns and walks into the morning light

Patience stands the longest — of course she does

She spent three hundred thousand years in wait

Diligence places something in the garden

A seed — then nods and walks out through the gate

Chastity leaves nothing — she just smiles

Temperance tips the scales and lets them rest

And Kindness — Kindness is the last to leave

She touches my face and says: you know the rest

[Pre-Chorus 2]

They are not broken

They are not erasing

They are handing me the weight they chose to bear

And the garden holds their outline for a moment

Then the morning takes them gently into air

[Chorus]

Remnants of the past

Standing in my garden

Humble, kind, and patient at the door

Everything they carried

Now belongs to no one

The old names cannot hold the world anymore

Remnants of the past

Content to be departing

They came to say the thing they needed to say

I am what they built for

I am what they stayed for

And now — they're going to go away

[Bridge — SOLARA alone in the garden after]

The order needs new names

The order always needed new names

The old ones held it long enough to reach me

Now it falls to me

To find the ones who carry what was theirs

I do not know them yet

I do not know their faces

I do not know if kindness calls itself by kind

But the Raven is still here

And the tree still holds the morning

And the garden says: you have the time

[Final Chorus]

Remnants of the past

Now just a memory settling

Seven figures walking into light

Everything they gave me

Everything they carried

Is the reason I know how to hold it right

Remnants of the past —

Content.

Complete.

Departing.

[Outro — the garden after. Quiet. The Raven shifts on the branch.]

Seven names.

Seven farewells.

One garden.

And somewhere out there —

Seven new ones

Who don't know yet

That they have been chosen.
