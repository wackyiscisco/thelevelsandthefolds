[Intro — march tempo. She is walking. Purpose in every step.]

[Verse 1 — controlled, almost cold. The mission is clear.]
I am done waiting in the ruin
I am done kneeling to the grey
I know her name now and I know the road
And I have nothing left to lose today
She is the dark queen — I have read the letter
I know exactly what she made me carry
I will not arrive with mercy in my hands
This time I am not coming with a prayer

[Pre-Chorus — harder now]
She took my faith
She took my station
She took the warmth I never knew was her
She turned the world to ash and walked away
Now I will find her — and we will settle this

[Chorus — driving forward, almost a war march]
I am walking down the path less taken
Not the road they built for me to follow
I am walking toward the thing that broke me
With a sword and nothing left to swallow
Let her armies find me on this road
Let her shadows think they've found their prize
I am walking down the path less taken
[low, cold]
And this time — I'm not afraid to fight

[Verse 2 — she senses them. The trap closing.]
I could hear them in the treeline first
The way they moved told me they were trained
I have done this long enough to read the sound
Of someone who was sent and has been paid
And then they stepped out and they said it proud:
"Her Malevolence will see us well rewarded"
[pause]
Wrong words
Wrong road
Wrong day

[Pre-Chorus 2 — the shift. Something colder coming.]
She sent them for me
After everything

She sent them to collect what she destroyed

[Chorus 2 — WAR takes over mid-section. Production shifts violently.]
I am walking down the path less taken
[beat]
And something in me has stopped asking questions
[beat]
Every face I lost — I see them on this road
Every prayer I burned — still burning
I am not the shield anymore — I am not the light
[clash]
I am the thing they should have feared from the beginning
[heavy guitar underneath]
[War Section — no pre-chorus. Just her voice and the drums.]
[spoken — flat, deadly]
My friends. My faith. My station. My years.
My god. My name. My warmth.
[pause]
All of it. Because of her. She is no light.
[beat]
When I find her — there will be no letter. No nun. No plea.
There will only be — this.
[the sounds of it. Then silence.]

[Bridge — stillness. One note. She looks down.]
[near silence]
[a single low string]
Blood on me is nothing new but -
The white was always wrong on me.
[barely]
...I think I like this color better. Crimson like the SUN.
(the drone hums — warm and wrong and deep)
[beat]
More of them.
[she doesn't move]
...Let them come.
[Final Section — she is held down. Then she sees her. Then she moves.]
There — at the edge —
She's in bleach white that used to be black
A word — a spell — the world just — stopped —
[silence for one beat]
[then the most forward-driving moment in the album — she charges]

**[Final Chorus — charging. Unstoppable. The last verse of the album before the end.]*
I am running down the path less taken
Toward the one who made me what I'm carrying
I am running toward the light that I buried
And the darkness in my hands is not complaining
She is right there — right there — within the reach
Of everything I have left and what I've lost
I am running down the path less taken
[final line — sword in hand, closing fast]
And mercy — ends — here

[Outro — the charge continues into silence. No resolution. The track ends before the sword lands.]
Let the darkness guide this strike.
