[Verse 1]

Six captains in one room

This had never happened before

For good reason

Solara: listening

Void: already calculating

Chaos: already standing like he'd been chosen

Madela: cleaning a corner

Asala: present — that was all

Fable: narrating the room to the room

"Someone needs to lead the assault," said Cassie

"Yes," said everyone

"Who," said Cassie

Chaos stepped forward

[Pre-Chorus]

And the speeches began

[Chorus]

Captain commander — who takes the point

Captain commander — who leads to 289

Who walks first through Level 280

Who calls the formation

Who the darkness looks at when we arrive

Captain commander

[CHAOS — The Speech]

"I have been to more wrong places than anyone in this room

and survived all of them"

Fable: "He speaks with the certainty of someone

who has never been right

and has somehow always been fine"

"I can hear you," said Chaos

"I know," said Fable — "I was narrating"

"I KNOW"

He finished the speech

It was long

It was confident

It was wrong on at least three points

But delivered very well

[VOID — The Bigger Speech]

Void stood

The room changed temperature

"I have walked levels that do not have names

fought beings that are not remembered

because the memory of them was too heavy to carry

I did not survive them — I ended them"

Long pause

"That is why it should be me"

Fable: "And the shadows behind him

shifted slightly to applaud

which was impressive

and also deeply unsettling"

Void looked at Fable

Fable: "I am the narrator. I go where the story goes."

[Madela interlude]

Madela cleaned the far corner

Then the window sill

Then a chair nobody had sat in

Then the floor around Rumble — which took longer

She did not look up once

This was somehow the most intimidating thing in the room

[Chorus]

[The impasse]

Asala had not spoken

"Asala," said Cassie

"I am thinking," said Asala

"What are you thinking"

"I am still thinking"

Solara said: "We decide later"

Chaos: "Or—"

"Later"

"What about an—"

"Chaos"

"An eating contest would resolve—"

"NO"

Unanimous

Even Fable agreed — and narrated her own agreement

They began to leave

The meeting had ended in nothing

This was expected

[Bridge — Helena]

Then the room changed again

Different from Void — colder, final

Helena

She did not sit

She did not need to

"The question is answered"

Nobody had asked her

"Solara leads the assault"

Chaos: "But—"

"So I can keep an eye on her"

Silence

Void looked at Solara

Solara looked at Helena

Helena looked at nothing in particular

That was somehow worse

Fable began to narrate

"The narrator," said Helena

Fable stopped

[Final Chorus]

Captain commander — it's decided

Captain commander — it's Solara

Not because they voted

Not because she asked

Because Helena is watching

And wants her where she can be seen

Captain commander — no choice

[Outro]

They looked at Solara

Solara looked at the door Helena had come through

The door was already gone

"Well," said Chaos

"I would have won the eating contest"

"Nobody doubted that," said Castor

"Thank you"

"It was not a compliment"

Solara: "We march in one week"

One week

Madela cleaned the window again

It was already clean

She cleaned it anyway
