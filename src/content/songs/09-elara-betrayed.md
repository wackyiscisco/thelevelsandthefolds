[Verse 1 — she's in the middle of the purge. The letter is in her hand.]
I was burning what was left of it
The last of what I called a life
The pages and the pictures and the promises
The doctrines and the doctrine's knife
And in the bottom of the ruin
In the ash of what I thought I knew
There was a letter I had never seen before
Addressed to someone — not to me — but to

[Pre-Chorus — voice quiet, stunned]
I should have kept burning
I should have let it go
I should have never opened it
(barely)
But I had to know

[Chorus — controlled, reading, each line landing harder]
There were two of them — that's what it said
There were two born from the same beginning
One to carry dark, one to carry light
One to end the war, one to keep it living
They were separated — that's what it said
To protect the greater — that's what they called it
Someone made a choice to split the thing in half
And then — built everything around it

[Verse 2 — the connections forming. The rage building slow.]
I have given every year I had in service
To the people who wrote this page
I have bled and held the line for the institution
Run by the same hands that built this cage
They knew — they always knew — there was another
They knew and they called it necessary
They took a child and drew a line between her and the other
And then they handed me a cross to carry

[Pre-Chorus 2 — quieter, more dangerous]
And told me this was chosen
And told me this was grace
And told me I was everything they needed
And never showed me any other face

[Chorus 2 — rage fully present now]
There were two of them — and they kept us blind
There were two walking toward each other all this time
One was burning churches — one was burning too
And the people who knew everything — said nothing — let it continue
They made a choice — that's what it said
For the greater good — that's what they called it
Someone held the warmth I'd been searching for my whole life
(voice breaks, just once)
In their hands
And withheld it

[Bridge — near silence. Just her voice and one piano note. The lullaby motif rises — the last time.]
(spoken, hollow)
There was a warmth I kept dreaming about.
I told myself it was grief.
I told myself it was the aftermath.
(the lullaby motif — soft, a few notes — the closest it's ever been)
She was real.
She has always been real.
And they knew.
(long pause)
(the drone rises — massive now — and buries the warmth completely)
(the lullaby motif is gone. It does not return.)
They knew — and they said nothing.[

Final Chorus — drone dominant. Something new and cold underneath. The rage has gone quiet — worse.]
There were two of them — and I was one
There were two and they decided we were better undone
I have been alone inside a lie they built for me
I have been a faithful soldier in a war they designed to keep me
From the only thing that ever — in any life — would have been enough
(final line, low, almost a whisper)
And they called that faith
(silence)
(the blade-note holds)

[Outro — drone and the blade-note. No warmth. No choir. Nothing left but the dark and the cold.]
(held)
(fades)
