[Intro - spoken, like a sermon]
In the beginning...
there were seven days.
Everyone knows about those.

### Verse 1
On the first day He spoke and the darkness ran clear
On the second He split what was sea from the air
On the third came the mountains, the rivers, the field
On the fourth came the stars and the sun and their wheel
On the fifth came the creatures that swim and that soar
On the sixth came the man made of dust and of more
On the seventh He rested and called it all good
But nobody asked what He knew that He should

### Pre-Chorus
There was one more day
Hidden, unspoken
Written in silence
Before it was broken

### Chorus
The eighth day was never recorded
The eighth day was never revealed
The eighth day He made what He shouldn't
And left it to quietly feed

### Verse 2
He reached into nothing and pulled something cold
A thing without a face and a thing without soul
He breathed it inside us and sealed up the seam
So deep that we'd never know what it could mean
It wore different names: greed, envy, the knife
It wore different faces but lived the same life
It waited in temples, in kingdoms, in thrones
It never stopped growing, it never grew old

### Pre-Chorus
There was one more truth
Locked in the scripture
Burned from the pages
Before you could read her

### Chorus
The eighth day was never recorded
The eighth day was never revealed
The eighth day He made what He shouldn't
And left it to quietly feed

### Bridge
We built our altars on it
We crowned our kings with it
We wrote our laws around it
We prayed on top of it
We called it civilization
We called it God's design
We called it human nature
But it was always His

### Final Chorus
The eighth day was never recorded
The eighth day was never revealed
The eighth day He made what He shouldn't
And left it to quietly feed
We built everything on it
We knelt before it
We named it holy
And it was never ours

### Outro - spoken, one line at a time, slow
On the first day... light.
On the second... the sea.
On the third... the land.
On the fourth... the stars.
On the fifth... the creatures.
On the sixth... you.
On the seventh... rest.
On the eighth... He never rested.
