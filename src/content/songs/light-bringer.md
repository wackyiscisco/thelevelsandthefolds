[Verse 1 - barely above a whisper, sparse piano, dark and low]
The blade found me where I stood
Between the vow and the understood
I felt it — clean, deliberate, done
Not the ending I'd become

The ground beneath me starts to split
And something ancient — chooses it
Not my power, not my hand
Something older took command

[Pre-Chorus - uncertain, building]
The fire I've carried since I can remember
Is cooling down to something tender
I don't know this feeling in my chest
Something leaving — something blessed

[Chorus - cinematic dark pop, heavy but cracking open]
Anger, hate — where did you go?
Anguish, malice — letting go
My eyes are open and the light —
I can see, and it burns right
Not the burn of something dying
This is something — clarifying
My heart is lighter, I'm not whole
But something wicked left my soul

[Verse 2 - stripped back, intimate, memory surfacing]
And then I hear it, somewhere far
A room I've never named, a scar
Before the robes, before the chains
Before they taught me how to hate

A crib, a child, a lamp above
The kind of room that once held love
And voices — sharp, conspiring low
They didn't know that I would know

[Pre-Chorus 2 - same melody, charged now with what she knows]
They said: hide her, what she carries
Not supposed to — can you hear this?
She was never meant to fall this far
They built the cage before the bars

[Chorus]
Anger, hate — where did you go?
Anguish, malice — letting go
My eyes are open and the light —
I can see, and it burns right
Not the burn of something dying
This is something — clarifying
My heart is lighter, I'm not whole
But something wicked left my soul

[Bridge - full theatrical reveal, choir optional, earth crack SFX]
From a distant hazy memory I heard this  -
"Hide this light
The light bringer must not come to pass
Feed her darkness
Feed her malevolence
And never — never —
Let her see the light"

[beat drop]

You built me wrong on purpose
You made me dark on purpose
Every wound, every whisper
Every night they called me sinister —

That was never who I was
Was it?
I was always the bringer
They just couldn't — let me be it

[Final Chorus - production lifts, warmer, strings open up]
Anger, hate — you found the door
Anguish, malice — not anymore
My eyes are open and the light —
Doesn't burn — it feels right
Not the end of something dying
This is something — rising
My heart is lighter, I'm not whole
But the wicked — left my soul

[Outro - solo vocal, almost alone with herself]
They called it a weapon
They buried it deep
But light doesn't leave you
It waits, while you sleep

So Am I the Light Bringer?
The Light Bringer?
Maybe.....but not yet.
