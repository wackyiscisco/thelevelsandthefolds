TITLE NOTE — STRUCTURAL / FORESHADOWING:  
Surface title function: Mary is internally denying that she wants Hansel there, while the final scene pivots the word “hair” into the unexplained hairy/furry figure that crashes through the library window.  
“Wish You Were Hair” echoes “Wish You Were Here.” The title does NOT identify the creature or establish any objective supernatural species/mechanic. At cut, Mary only observes what may be hair or fur in darkness.
